Apple's two parameters, damping and response, sampled into CSS linear() by the board's build, so the curve drawn is the curve that runs. Settle is the house default: no overshoot. Only the sheet overshoots, by 0.6 %, which is Apple's own drawer value (0.8 / 0.3) rounded toward calm.

Routes and the shared photograph

  • One view-transition-name per model: the picker tile's photograph becomes the build's stage, the finale's boat and the paper's cover, on a settle spring of 1.0 / 0.4 s (588 ms). TanStack Router starts the transition on the navigations it owns.
  • The glass sheet materialises after the photograph lands, 80 ms behind it, so glass is never seen sliding over a picture that is still moving.
  • Routes crossfade underneath in 200 ms. The pill's lit door travels to its new door (motion's layoutId).
  • The price is named apart with its animation off. It is never part of a morph and never counts.

How it reflows

  • 390 the pill goes to the foot as glyphs, the lit door keeping its word; the stage takes the width with the sheet over its foot; the motor tiles stack; the toast stands above the pill.
  • 834 two columns: the stage and its sheet on the left, the chapters on the right; the pill stays at the head.
  • 1920 the three columns keep their measure and the stage takes the rest; the sheet stops at 560 px so the figure never drifts from its level.

What Northside can change, and the kit stays designed

  • Accent re-lights every rim, glow, keycap and the water in one token.
  • Mark takes the plate's centre under the same cone of light; it is shown, never recoloured.
  • Pictures the stage takes any held photograph; words on it sit on the veil at 70 %, measured on the pixels.

Keyboard: every control is a button, link or input with the focus ring; the refused act stays focusable and says why. Reduced motion: colour and opacity stay, the sink, travel and morph go.

What only this kit does: depth. A control is an object with a top edge and a shadow, and state is light on its rim.
