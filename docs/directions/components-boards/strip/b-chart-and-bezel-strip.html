Emil Kowalski's frame plots stiffness 100, damping 10, mass 1 (craft/emil-great-animations.png), which is a damping ratio of 0.5 and a response of 0.63 s: it overshoots by 16 % and takes 1.3 s to settle. An instrument does not do that. Every part that travels here clicks into place on one critically damped spring in 294 ms.

The bold tech: the build as a scroll-driven chapter

  • Live on the board: scroll the build's column. A CSS scroll timeline fills one dash per chapter at its foot and the foot names the chapter, 02 / 04 · motor. The dash is 1.1 KB of CSS (0.5 KB gzip) and 0 bytes of script; it runs on the compositor.
  • In the app: the native timeline where the browser has it. GSAP ScrollTrigger holds the stage while the chapters pass and snaps a chapter home, which CSS cannot: gsap 28.4 KB + ScrollTrigger 18.0 KB gzip, measured in node_modules. Lenis smooths the wheel on a desk, 5.4 KB gzip.
  • On a phone: Lenis is off (the thumb's own momentum is better), so the phone pays the timeline and ScrollTrigger only. Lenis is also off under reduced motion and while a caret is in the option search.
  • Keyboard: every chapter head stays a button; Tab and Enter never scroll-jack.

Routes and the shared photograph

  • A route is a 150 ms crossfade, the way an instrument changes pages. One element travels: the model's photograph, picker tile → the build's stage → the paper's cover, on the detent spring.
  • The door's lit underline slides to the new door on the detent. The price is named apart and does not travel.
  • Reduced motion keeps the crossfade and drops the travel.

What Northside can change, and the kit stays designed

  • Accent re-inks readings, cursors, brackets and dashes.
  • Mark takes the centre of the neatline, which frames anything.

Keyboard: the bezel is a radio group (in the app, Base UI moves it with the arrows), heads and tiles are buttons, and every one wears the app's focus ring. Reduced motion: colour and opacity stay; the scroll fill stays too, because it is the reader's own scroll position, not motion the app adds.

What only this kit does: every figure is drawn to scale from the file's own cells — the deadrise as its 24° angle, the power band on its range, Cash and Trade as the two ends of one ruler.
