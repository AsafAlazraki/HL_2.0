Levels · the strip beneath the board

C · The fence — grouped by audience

The price file split by who may read it. Northside’s side is a dark ground and the customer’s is white paper, with a striped fence between them. The fence is the one place a column can cross, and the only thing standing on it is the column the owner must decide.

The five questions, on this board

Matrix
C draws no list × rung grid. Each rung row holds the file’s own names for it, each with its reach: “Cash · 7 boat lists · 792 priced”, “Sell inc Rego · 8 trailer makers · 441”. The 25 lists appear as names under the rung, so there are four rows, not 125 cells.
No Trade
It is a dashed name in the Trade row: “→ their first rung · Trailers, Parts, 8 Jeanneau”.
The write
Crossing writes no cell. Before, the card says what it opens: 209 motor lines may print it if the owner then chooses it, and one undo puts it back. The cell writes are A’s Set, with the same census and toast.
Difference
On the dark side, Warranty, Cash and Landed stand as three figures in a row. On the white side the paper prints one figure and names no rung. A trade quote prints $45,932 the same way, and only this screen names the rung.
Boundary
The boundary is the whole composition. The 135 cost names stand as chips under the engine’s own refusal sentence, and the foot says where this side is read: on the sheet and this screen only.

What Northside changes from its own settings

  • Which side each rung sits on, never the side of a cost column.
  • The names of the rungs, and which specimen the paper prints.
  • Whether Not priced lists show on this screen or only in the configurator’s warnings.
