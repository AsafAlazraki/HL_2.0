Levels · the strip beneath the board

A · The ladder — rungs down, lists across

The price file read as one ladder: the rungs run down the left, the lists run across, and each cell names the column a customer pays, or says it has none and reads Cash. The fence is drawn through the ladder itself, and a pressed cell opens at the foot with its count beside Set.

The five questions, on this board

Matrix
14 lists across, 5 rungs down. The 8 identical trailer makers share one column, and so do the 2 factory package lists. Labour, Oils and Registration declare no level and sit in a foot line. The cells hold column names, not prices, so a rung reads in one look. Solid: a column of the file. Dashed: none. Ochre: Not priced. Grey: Northside only. Hatched: cost.
No Trade
The empty cell says where it goes: “→ Sell inc Rego”, “→ Sell”, “8 read Cash”. That is the rung priceAtLevel actually resolves to, so a gap reads as a fact and not as a hole.
The write
Before: the census sits beside Set: 91 Trade cells written, 91 hold Cash today, 0 blank, 1 undo. After: the store’s toast, “Trade set … on 91 boats in Stacer · Undo”, pinned to the one batch entry. Each written cell ticks once.
Difference
The figure comes first and the share is the word that explains it. “$1,730 · = Cash · 100%” in the sheet, and “95% of Cash on all 588” in the cell. A percentage is read off the columns and never stored against a moving base.
Boundary
A black rule crosses the ladder. Cash, Trade and Fitted sit above it, and Warranty and Cost below. Every cell under the rule is grey or hatched. The flag “May reach a customer” sits in the opened sheet.

How it reflows

390
Kinds become chips. Each list is a card whose rungs are label rows, with the fence inside the card, and Highfield, Stabicraft and Haines fold to one line each. The opened cell is a bottom sheet with Set at thumb height.
834
The kinds become tabs over the grid, so the 7 boat columns stand at 100px. The sheet docks underneath at full width in two columns: facts over figures, then the act over the flag.
1920
All 14 columns stand, and Services opens into its own 3 columns. Each cell gains its specimen figure on a second line. The sheet keeps four columns at a 1,600px measure.

What Northside changes from its own settings

  • Rung labels and their order. A rung that may not reach a customer is refused first, because the first rung is the fallback.
  • Which column each rung reads, list by list, and what it contains (registration, install), each with its cell.
  • The may-reach-a-customer flag, which is off for a rung worked from cost.
  • Which kind shows first, and whether identical lists fold into one column.

Its silhouette against the built screens

  • Data is one kind-grouped list of rows with counts, under seven maker cards. A is a grid read down columns, with the rung names (not list names) at the left and a docked sheet at the foot.
  • The sheet shows one list’s rows × its columns, full of figures. A holds the names of columns in a 14 × 5 grid, with almost no figures.
  • Quotes is a ledger with a side panel. A has no side panel; the opened cell docks below the grid with a crosshair.
  • Against this round: Rules B is bands × three owner columns of tall cards, with no crosshair, fence or hatching.
