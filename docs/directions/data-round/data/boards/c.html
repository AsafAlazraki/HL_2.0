Ask the file

The one job

The Master Price File as one place: what needs Northside, what is new, the rooms, and every list it carries, each a door (docs/SCREENS.md, the owner on 2026-10-31).

What only this screen does

It is the only screen that answers “where is it?” across the whole file at once. Makers, the 28 lists of what fits, sections and lines are counted by kind in one answer. It also draws the 28 lists of what fits as what they are: a table of each maker against what it is paired with. The bar’s Find answers for a quote, Fitment for one boat. Only Data answers for the file.

Order and grouping

  1. 1. A rail at the left: the title, the file’s stamp, and the six rooms as counted rows (“The file, since it landed” lit).
  2. 2. The find, as the head of the page and the widest control on it.
  3. 3. The pulse: six counted cells since 16 September 2026, each a door, then one sentence with every figure in bold.
  4. 4. The holdings under ruled sentence heads: the makers, then the matrix of the 28 lists of what fits (makers against motors, trailers, Dealer Fit and P/D parts), then the 18 lists you sell from in their groups.
  5. Typed into, the rail becomes the answer’s kinds and the main column becomes the answer (drawn below).

The mark

The marks appear twice, and at two jobs. They sit in a row of seven tiles under the makers’ ruled head, and at 20 px down the matrix’s first column, where they name each row. A wordless matrix would make a reader decode a code, so the mark is the row label. Stabicraft is set as a word.

What a dealer can replace

The accent (the rail’s lit bar, the doors and the room glyphs) and the kind inks in the matrix’s heads. The figures carry the page, so there is no photograph to lose.

Motion

Typing a first letter does three things. The pulse fades out over 120 ms. The rail’s rooms crossfade to the answer’s kinds over 160 ms, in place, with nothing sliding. The answer’s groups appear in their final positions with no stagger. Clearing the field reverses it. The rail never moves, and the find stays put (sticky at 390). A count that changes swaps in place. Reduced motion: no fades, only the swap.

Keyboard

/ or typing from anywhere lands in the find. ↓ moves from the field into the answer’s kinds, then into its results. Esc clears the field and returns to the pulse. At rest, Tab goes from the find to the six cells, then the matrix (a grid, arrows inside), then the lists.

Honest constraints

  • The F115 answer is counted over every list’s own words, codes included: 306 lines. The build counts through src/domain/catalogue/search, and the board says this answer is the file’s own text and not an index.
  • “Nothing on the price list is a maker or a section called F115” is true. The answer says what it is not, as well as what it is.
  • The cells are doors, never trends. There is no arrow and no “since last week”, because the file has one date.
  • No cost or margin appears in the answer. The motors table shows code, name, shaft and steering, the file’s own facts.

Its risk

The needs read as figures, not as a list to finish. Nothing turns to a tick, and a dealer sees 37 beside 0 without being told which matters more. The sticky rail also makes the screen a two-part layout that other screens do not have.
