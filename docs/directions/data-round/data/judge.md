# Data, judged — data round, 2026-11-03

A fresh judge who had never seen the screen. Served from its own build (`e2e/out/dist-6230`, `vite preview --port 6230`), reached through the real door with the real Master Price File (the `throughTheDoor` walk: a name at Entry, *Load the Master Price File*, then `/data`), in a fresh browser context at each of the six sizes. Every screenshot named below is in `before/`, and its file name starts with its size (`390x844-`, `844x390-`, `834x1112-`, `1280x800-`, `1440x900-`, `1920x1080-`). At every size and state I measured sideways scroll, text cut by its own box, text under 12 px, rows in view, element boxes and page errors. The one list made during the walk was made by pressing the act and typing a name, and it was then undone. Nothing was planted. The server was stopped before this was written.

**Verdict: REDRAW.** The screen is a good *register of containers*: seven maker plates, eighteen rows, and a spread that opens in place. It counts honestly, and it holds 18 of 18 rows at 1280×800. But the job the owner gives this screen is *the Master Price File as one place: every list it carries, the makers, the rooms, what is new and what needs Northside, and the door to each*. The composition has no place for two of those five things. It shows nothing that is new, and it shows nothing that needs Northside apart from one pill ("37 to settle"). It draws the six rooms as a 574 px nav strip, though they are where most of the price file's work happens. It draws the 28 lists of what fits which boat only as 11 px glyph lines. On a phone the lists start 1,426 px down, under seven plates. At 1920 a quarter of the window is empty, and the right half of every row is blank. The find field says "Nothing on the price list is called 'Highfield'" directly under the Highfield plate. Changing the finish would not fix these. The front page needs a new composition, one that leads with the price file's state and makes the rooms real doors. The spread is worth keeping in that composition, and the mammoth work is listed in §3.

## 4. The verdict

**REDRAW.** The composition is a register of containers: plates over rows, with a spread in place. It is right for "what lists exist" and thin for the owner's whole job, because it has no place for *what is new* or *what needs Northside*. It treats the six rooms as a nav strip, it hides more than half the file's lines as glyphs, and on a phone it puts the lists under seven plates. The next round needs a fresh sweep: Linear's project overview, Stripe's dashboard home, GitHub Insights, Airtable's base home, and a dealer DMS's price-file screen if one can be reached. Then three drawn directions, each leading with state above inventory and making the rooms real doors, and each saying how it reflows at 390, 844 and 1920. **Keep:** the spread's content (series bars that add back to the count, what goes with it as tiles, *Where from*, Prices in the sale's names), the honest counting through `register.ts`, the one-write-with-Undo, the shelf's one-tab-stop keyboard, and reduced motion. **Fix now, whatever the direction:** L3 (390 sideways scroll), L8 (an empty name makes "Custom table") and L9 (the spread under the bar at 844×390).
