Highfield’s settings
Board C · a maker's own page

The spec sheet

The screen's one job: one maker as Northside sells it: its line, its mark, its photographs and colour, its models and what each carries.

What only this screen does. It answers the second question at the desk on the page, “the 600 or the 660?”: two or three models of one maker set side by side, row by row, with every row that is the same quieted. No other screen in the app sets two boats beside each other.

Order and grouping

  • A head that is three figures for the maker (67 models · 2.3–9.2 m · $2,770–$198,610 at Cash), the same height with or without a photograph.
  • The series as one segmented row (?series=Sport).
  • Left, the ladder: the series' models by length, each rung its people, horsepower, a bar for its length and its from-price; tick up to three (?compare=).
  • Right, the spec sheet: the ticked models in columns, banded Price → Specifications → What fits, with Highlight differences on. Each column's own What fits and Quote.
  • Then prices by series, quotes, changes, the places it goes with and Settings.

References new to this board

  • Porsche's compare picker (live/porsche-compare-select.png): models chosen into named slots from a list with counts, the compare disabled until two are chosen.
  • Arc's model head (live/arc-sport.png): three figures under the name and nothing else.
  • Also: live/quicksilver-605-open-specs.png (banded label and value), live/apple-iphone-compare-rows.png (a dash for absent, never a blank), live/carexpert-specs-scrolled.png (Highlight differences), live/honda-marine-scrolled.png (families by their HP span, for Yamaha).

No earlier board cites Porsche's compare or Arc.

The mark and the photograph

The mark is the showpiece on the left of the head; the three figures stand where the photograph stood, so 18 places with no photograph lose nothing. The 18 photographs are one press (“18 on the water”) into the lightbox. The models are seen in the sheet's column heads, each its own catalogue render at one scale.

What a dealer can replace

Ground, mark ink, line and photograph, as now. The ladder's bar and the lit rows read the room's blues, so a new ground changes only the head.

Where it is weak

The first screen shows 16 models as rungs of text, not 16 pictures: the customer at the desk sees figures before boats. On a phone the sheet sits under the ladder, so a compare is a scroll away.

Motion

Highlight differences: the quiet rows' ink fades in --duration-hover. The ladder never re-orders under the pointer.

Reduced motion: cuts.

Keyboard: the series row (arrow keys), the ladder's ticks in order, then the sheet's column acts, then the plates. Space on a rung ticks it.
