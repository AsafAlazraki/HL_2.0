The lookup

The screen's one job: one maker as Northside sells it: its line, its mark, its photographs and colour, its models and what each carries.

What only this screen does. It reads everything one model carries, across every list that fits it (motors, trailers, dealer fit, parts), as one answer with its count, without changing anything. Fitment answers the same question for one boat in order to change it (“Take it off”, “Put it on”); the picker answers nothing about fit. The risk is plain: B is the closest of the three to an existing screen, and a judge may call its spine Fitment's.

Order and grouping

  • One compact head for every place, photograph or not: mark, 67 models in 7 series, the span, n of n priced, the price list's date (as Rhino-Rack stamps its data), the act and the gallery.
  • Left, the question: a find, then the series, each folded to its count and from-price; the open series lists its models with their from-price (the range is here, as a list).
  • Right, the answer: the model's own photograph and its three figures; then “16 things fit the Sport 660” as the title, the facets, and kit cards grouped motors → trailers → dealer fit → parts, the recommended motor and trailer marked.
  • A census of the maker across the price list, then prices, quotes and Settings.
  • The model is in the address (?model=boat_highfield:513); a facet is ?only=motors.
