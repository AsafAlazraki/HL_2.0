The line-up

Recommended

The screen's one job (docs/SCREENS.md, the judge): one maker as Northside sells it: its line, its mark, its photographs and colour, its models and what each carries.

What only this screen does. It is the one place every model of one maker stands with its price, its three facts and what it carries, and where two or three of them are set side by side. The picker shows a picture and a from-price to start a quote; Fitment changes what goes on one boat; the sheet edits lines. None of them answers “which Highfield is right for this customer” on one page.

Order and grouping

  • A short livery head: the mark, 67 models in 7 series, the span $2,770 to $198,610 at Cash, 2.3 to 9.2 m, 588 of 588 versions priced, the act, and the gallery of 18 photographs as one press.
  • The seven series as picture tiles, plus Every model. A tile filters in place; the series is in the address (?series=Sport).
  • The models of the series, one card each, in the price list's own order (Price and Length re-order it, ?order=).
  • One sentence, once, that standard inclusions are not held.
  • Then, after the range and never before it: prices by series, quotes, the places it goes with, what changed, and Settings.
  • The compare tray in the range's own head, beside the order, filled by each card's Compare (?compare=); the range head docks under the bar once the page scrolls, so the tray is always one press away and never floats over a card.

A card

Its own catalogue picture where the ledger holds one: 46 of Highfield's 67 models. Its spoken name and Model Code, how many versions and in what. From $X at Cash, or one figure where every version costs the same. Length · people · horsepower as the file writes them. Then what it carries, from the fitment reading: the recommended motor where the join marks one (52 of 67), then motors, trailers, dealer fit and parts counted. Three presses: Compare, What fits (/fitment?boat= that model, one press, not the chooser) and Quote it (/quote/new?brand=boat_highfield&model=).

A card with no picture (Sport 700 WL, Sport 760 WL, Patrol 600 …) is typographic by design: its length and horsepower set large in the well, and “No picture of the Sport 700 WL is held”. Never a sibling's picture, never a grey box, the same height as its neighbours.

The mark, and the head

The mark stays the showpiece, in white ink on Highfield's cobalt at one optical size, and the name is not printed under it again (the judge's “name twice”). The head is 220 px, not 300: the span sits where the empty right 60% was. For the 18 places with no photograph, the same head is drawn without the picture and needs no band of empty colour (Jeanneau, Yamaha and Labour Rates below).

What a dealer can replace

The ground (one of eight bounded colours), the mark's ink, the photograph and its line: all four from Settings, unchanged. The cards read only the price list and the ledger, so a new photograph changes the head and nothing else.

Motion

A series tile pressed: the grid's cards leave by fading 8 px down in --duration-exit, the new series' cards arrive by fading 8 px up in --duration-enter, 20 ms apart, the first eight only. The head, the tiles and the tray stay still. A card's Compare: the chip flies nothing; it appears in the tray with --duration-pop and the card's ring draws in --duration-press. The compare sheet opens from the foot in --duration-sheet on --ease-drawer.

Reduced motion: every change is a cut; the tray's count changes in place.

Keyboard: Back, the act, the gallery, the series tiles (arrow keys move between them), then the cards in reading order, each card's Compare, What fits and Quote it, then the plates after the range come last, so focus never goes right before it goes down.

Honest constraints

  • Every figure is read through the app's own readers (boards/extract.facts.ts): the picker's fleetOf for models, from-prices and series; readBoatFit for what each carries; readPlace for the livery and partners; pictureBook for pictures.
  • No cost and no margin is read: Base Cost, Landed Hull Cost, Nett CTD and MU % never reach the board. Trade is a declared price level, shown on a staff page; the compare sheet is a staff surface too.
  • The Max People column is filled on 567 of Highfield's 588 versions, not all of them; SP300 carries none, and its card says so where the figure would be. Min HP is on 581 of 588 (ADV7 has none: “up to 250 hp”).
  • 4 Jeanneau models are filed under no series in the price list: DB 37 OB, DB 43 OB, TH33, TH38. They stand here under their own heading, so the 27 in the head are the 27 drawn.
  • Labour Rates names no price level, so no rate is printed here. Its Rate (inc GST) column is not declared as what a customer pays, and Prices does not carry Labour Rates yet.
  • Highfield's pictures are Highfield's own renders and photographs, each with its ledger entry; Jeanneau's are the price file's own Image Link copies (the row's own picture), with no person-marked hero, so its head has no photograph.
  • No quote is held in the seed, so Quotes is drawn empty: that is the true state.
