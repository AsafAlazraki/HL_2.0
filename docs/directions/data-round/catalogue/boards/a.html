The aisle as a page

The screen’s one job (docs/SCREENS.md, the chandlery): let a salesperson with a customer at the desk find a part by what it is for, see what it costs supply and fitted, and put it on a quote; and let Northside keep the list it shows.

What this screen does that no other does: it gives each of the 19 aisles its own page and address, which a salesperson can send, open on a phone at the boat, or keep in a tab; and on that page it puts first what the price list pairs with the customer’s boat. No other screen sorts parts by what they are for.

Order and grouping

  1. — A way back to every aisle, then the aisle’s name large, with its parts, headings and pictures counted.
  2. — The docked head: find, Pictured | All, suppliers as chips, the boat as a chip. It stays in view while the parts scroll.
  3. — Made for this boat: the parts the P/D list pairs with the chosen boat, in this aisle first.
  4. — The file’s own headings as sections, in file order, each counted. In a section, pictured parts are cards and come first; the rest are ruled lines.
  5. — A line opens in place: its heading, how it is fitted, the boats it is paired with, and three doors (its line in the sheet, Northside’s own picture, a quote).

The two prices

Every line says both of the file’s declared levels, labelled: supply (parts.l, Sell) and fitted (parts.y, Sell inc Install). They differ on 1,882 of 2,238 parts. 101 parts are priced fitted only and read fitted only with the fitted figure, never $0: Trailer Adjustment · 15 to 17ft reads $206 fitted. Where the Install Type is Supply Only, the fitted cell says so. 25 parts have no price at either level and say so with the door to Review. 15 carry $0 at both levels, as the file writes them (Supplied with Motor).

Hours are not drawn. The judge asked for TTF hours on the card, but the manifest declares parts.o (TTF hours) a cost column with P&A, CTD, MU, Base List, Labour $ and Total CTD. So hours stay in the sheet and never reach a surface a customer reads. The Install Type (parts.n, not a cost column) is shown when a line is opened.

The mark

Northside’s own mark heads the door: the chandlery is its shop. Inside an aisle the mark gives way to the aisle’s name, which is what the person needs at that point. Maker marks appear only where the marks ledger holds that maker’s (Yamaha, Highfield, ePropulsion). Garmin, Lowrance, GME and the rest are words, never a stand-in.

What a dealer can replace

The mark on the door, the accent, the aisle names and which headings each gathers (a Northside setting; Not sorted yet empties as headings are placed), the supplier names, and every picture. With no pictures at all, the page is the same lines and loses nothing.

Motion

Pressing an aisle on the door: the page is replaced and the window starts at the aisle’s top, so the result is under the person’s eye at every size. The docked head does not animate: it is position, not motion (Apple’s chip row). Once the aisle’s name has scrolled under it, the dock’s first row gains the name in small capitals (Anchoring & mooring · LSM · Stacer 529 Assault Pro), so the person always knows where they are. Opening a line: the line’s own panel grows under it over 180 ms with the kit’s settle curve; nothing else on the page moves except what is below it. The supplier chips press at 0.96.

Reduced motion: the panel appears at its full height, with no growth.

Keyboard: / to find; Tab reaches the back door, find, Pictured | All, each chip, the boat chip, then each line’s open button in order; Escape closes an opened line and returns the caret to its button.

Honest constraints

  • — Anchoring holds no pictured part, so the page drawn is lines only. That is the truth for 15 of 19 aisles.
  • — Supplier codes stand as the file writes them (LSM, SAW, BBB) until Northside names them. Naming is a list in Data, set once, with Undo, never guessed.
  • — The P/D lists pair 41 parts on sale with boats, 2,561 pairings in 6 lists (measured: 42 distinct part rows; the judge’s 75 was not reproduced). So Made for this boat is short, and the boat is a chip, never the door.
  • — Quoting a part on its own is off for Parts & Accessories on day one (letQuote). The refusal is one sentence in the opened line, with the switch.
  • — Cost, margin and hours appear on no surface of this board.
