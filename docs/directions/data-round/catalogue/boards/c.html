Board C · the chandlery redrawn

Two rooms: Show and Keep

The screen’s one job: show a customer at the desk what Northside sells, as well as it can be shown, and keep the list behind it whole.

What this screen does that no other does: it separates what the customer sees from the work the list needs, over the same aisles, with one control. Show has no code, no supplier and no Add a picture; Keep has every one of them, as six counted worklists that fall as the work is done.

Order and grouping

  1. — Northside’s mark, the room (Show | Keep) and find, at the top of both rooms.
  2. — Show: the aisle held large with Change aisle; its photographs as large cards; then every other part as type, grouped by heading, with its two prices.
  3. — Keep: grouped by the work, not by the aisle: to picture (by aisle, with the drop by code), to place, to name, to price, notes, and the quote switch.

To quote on its own

Parts & Accessories: off

Until it is on, a part is sold only on a quote that has a boat. One press, with Undo.

The two prices

On Show every part says supply and fitted in words beside the figures. They differ on 1,882 of 2,238. 101 read fitted only and their fitted figure, never $0: Trailer Adjustment · 15 to 17ft reads $206 fitted. Supply only is said where the Install Type says so. 25 with no price at either level are not shown on Show at all; they stand in Keep’s To price, so a customer never reads “no price”. 15 carry $0 at both levels, as the file writes them. TTF hours are a cost column and never drawn.

The mark

Northside’s mark leads both rooms at the size of a shop sign, because Show is the room a customer looks at: the chandlery is Northside’s shop, and Show is its window. Keep keeps the mark so the two rooms read as one place.

What a dealer can replace

The mark, the accent, the aisle names and their headings, the supplier names, and every picture. With no pictures at all, Show is the type lists, and the room still reads as a price list set well. Show and Keep themselves are fixed: they are the honesty of the screen.

Honest constraints

  • — Only 4 of 19 aisles hold a photograph: Sounders 161, Electric 14, Radios 12, Stereo 10. Show for Anchoring is type only, the truth.
  • — Fitted on the handheld radios equals supply, as the file writes it.
  • — Supplier names are proposals measured off the headings; nothing is named without Northside.
  • — Cost, margin and hours appear on no surface of this board; Keep shows codes and supplier codes, never cost.

Motion

Changing room: the segment’s thumb slides 180 ms on the kit’s settle curve; the room below is replaced, not animated. Change aisle opens the 19 aisles as a sheet over the room (a dialog at a desk), each with its photographs counted; choosing one closes it and the room starts at the aisle’s top. A card’s expand opens the gallery. In Keep a count falls as a picture lands, without a flourish: the number changes and the aisle’s bar fills.

Reduced motion: the thumb jumps; the sheet appears in place.

Keyboard: / finds; the room control is a radio group (arrows move); Tab then reaches Change aisle, the boat chip, each card’s expand, and in Keep each list’s door.
