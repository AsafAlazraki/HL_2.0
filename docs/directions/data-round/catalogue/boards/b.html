Board B · the chandlery redrawn

The rail and the ledger

The screen’s one job: find a part by what it is for, read its supply and fitted price, and act on it: quote it, picture it, or open its line.

What this screen does that no other does: it reads the whole parts list as one ledger that keeps its place, so the 447 lines of Propellers & motor care are walked in one table, sorted by any column, under the file’s own ten headings, with no Show the next. The sheet (/data/parts) shows the file’s columns; this shows the parts a person sells, by aisle.

Order and grouping

  1. — Find for the whole chandlery: a name, a code, a supplier or a heading.
  2. — The rail: Northside’s mark, the 19 aisles counted, the open aisle’s headings counted under it.
  3. — The ledger’s tools, which dock: the aisle and its counts, sort, All | With a picture | Without, supplier chips, the boat chip, and the one proposal about suppliers.
  4. — One table: a group row per heading, then a row per part: picture where held (no box where not), name, code, supplier, supply, fitted, and its line.

The two prices

Supply and fitted are two sortable columns, so “the cheapest fitted” is one press. They differ on 1,882 of 2,238 parts. 101 parts read fitted only in the supply column and their figure in the fitted one; Supply only is said where the Install Type says so; 25 with no price say so with the door to Review; 15 carry $0 at both levels as the file writes them. TTF hours are a declared cost column and are never drawn.

The mark

Northside’s mark heads the rail, at every desk size, above the aisles it owns. On a phone the rail is a sheet and the mark heads the sheet. Maker marks are not used in the ledger: a row is read, not branded.

What a dealer can replace

The mark, the accent, the aisle names and what each gathers, the supplier names, the columns shown (a Northside setting: code and supplier can be hidden at the desk), and the pictures. With no pictures at all the thumbnail column is simply empty and the ledger reads the same.

Honest constraints

  • — Propellers & motor care holds no pictured part, so its thumbnail column is empty as drawn. Sounders holds 161.
  • — Yamaha service itiems is the file’s spelling; correcting it is Review’s.
  • — The two NB: lines are notes in the price list and are drawn as notes, never as parts with a price.
  • — Supplier codes stand until named. YAM and Yam are proposed as one, never merged without Northside.
  • — Cost, margin and hours appear on no surface of this board.

Motion

Nothing animates in the ledger. Pressing an aisle in the rail replaces the table and starts it at its first row; pressing a heading scrolls the table to its group row, and the group row docks under the tools while its rows pass. A column head’s sort is instant. On a phone the rail rises as a sheet from the foot over 240 ms, and the window behind it does not move.

Reduced motion: the sheet appears without rising; scrolling to a heading jumps.

Keyboard: / finds; the rail is a list of links in Tab order; in the table, Tab reaches each column’s sort and each row’s Line; Enter on a part’s name opens its row’s acts (picture, quote).
