The build’s one job

One chapter per decision with a live price, a search reaching every chapter, every refusal with its reason (docs/SCREENS.md, Configurator). Drawn on chapter 04 beside a live outline of the printed quote.

What only this direction does

The salesperson builds the quote and its printed pages in one place: sections are cards that reorder, or move across to “kept off”, by drag or by press. The cover is the boat’s own photograph.

Against the original’s three floors

  • Configuring visually. Every colourway is a picture card with its own render and swatches, and the chosen one becomes the paper’s cover.
  • A dealer’s own picture. The empty square on a card is the way in, the same size as a filled one, so no card changes height (walk item 1).
  • The catalogue. Bands say their count before they open; a rigging kit for a motor not on the quote says so and links to the motor, never offered as if it fitted.

Motion, as choreography

Dragging a part toward the paper: the matching section card grows to 56px and takes the drop wash in 120ms; on drop the section’s line count ticks and the card settles. Reordering a section: the others slide 40px in 180ms to open its place. Reduced motion: sections swap in place; the wash and the count change.

Keyboard

Space lifts a card, ← goes to the printed quote, ↑ ↓ choose the section, Space drops (“GX700W, over Parts & fit-out, line 4 of 4”). The tick on a card is the one-press add. A section’s menu: “Put it on, after Price & GST”, “Move up”, “Keep it off”.

The honest constraints

  • The four made-for-this-boat parts have no picture: four equal empty squares.
  • Section choice belongs to Milestone 5’s paper (paper-handoff.md item 4); this direction pulls it into the build, which is its weight.
  • “Display only shown” is the ledger’s own note on Garmin and Lowrance pictures that leave out the transducer.
  • Rigging kits carry no chosen price: said, with “Choose on Prices”.
  • Never printed whatever is chosen: part numbers, workshop notes, cost.
