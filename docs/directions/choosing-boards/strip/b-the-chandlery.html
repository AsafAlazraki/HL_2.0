Against the original’s three floors

Why recommended

It is the simplest: one catalogue in two places, no new setting, every aisle measured off the file. Take two small grafts from C: the rigging kit’s condition line, and the empty picture square kept at full height.

Motion, as choreography

Pressing an aisle: its tile takes the chosen wash at once and the grid below cross-fades in 160ms; the aisle row does not move. Drag: the tile lifts with the lift shadow, the tray’s drop slot fills with the drop wash; on drop the tile flies to the slot in 220ms and becomes a line with its thumbnail, the total ticks. Reduced motion: no flight; the line appears and the slot’s wash fades.

Keyboard

Tab reaches the boat chip, the search, the aisles, then the tiles. Space lifts a tile, → goes to the quote, Space drops, Esc returns. “Add” does the same in one press and turns into “On the quote ✓ · Take off”.

The dropdown the walk named, replaced

“Show all 2,238 in Parts & Accessories” becomes 18 aisle tiles with drawings and counts; brand filters are pressable chips (GME | 42, Garmin | 5); the search takes words, never codes.

What fits this boat, one press away

The chip is the answer, and “What fits” beside it opens /fitment on this boat.

How it reflows

What Northside can replace

A picture on any part or aisle, the aisles’ words (the grouping is a pure function of the file’s headings; renaming is Northside’s), the accent and act colours.

The honest constraints

  • The four parts made for the 529 Assault Pro (Tournament) have no picture; they draw the aisle’s drawing, labelled, never a stand-in.
  • Counts are measured: 2,238 live parts in 18 aisles plus 28 not sorted (25 with no section, 3 DFO kits); 197 pictured, 161 of them sounders.
  • Dealer-fit packages (1,576) and rigging kits (615) have no maker’s picture and never borrow a part’s.
  • The Sport 560’s PVC and Hypalon rows share one picture; the board says it shows colour, not material. I-B-C prints as its code.
  • Cash level only; cost and margin are on no screen here.

New quote, in this direction

“What is being sold?” as four doors: a boat (largest, with marks), a motor, a trailer, or parts & fit-out. Parts-only opens this chandlery with the chip empty; a motor-only quote opens the motor aisle the same way.

Risk

Aisles are only as good as their words: the 18 names need Northside’s eye before they ship.
