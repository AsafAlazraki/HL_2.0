What only this one does

Northside is the showpiece. Its white mark spans 420px across Northside’s own water, the shader the app runs (src/ui/water.ts) stopped on its first frame. The boat is handed over the band’s foot on the kit’s plate. It is the only cover whose ground is drawn, not held, so the top 38% of the page never runs out of pixels and follows Northside’s accent.

What Northside replaces

The accent moves the band, the water (--water-deep and --water-lit derive from it), the summary’s ground and the total’s ink. The mark is the organisation’s white mark in marks-ledger.json. Any picture sits on the plate: a scene, a studio motor or a render. With none, the band and the name carry the page.

Type

Mark 420px. Reference 11px caps and 12px on the band (blue-50, 5.14 : 1 against the brightest pixel behind it, measured). Name 40px/300, finish 16px, facts 18px, customer 26px, total 48px/300 in the file’s blue.

Motion, keys, reduced motion

On screen the water stays still: this is paper. The build’s photograph morphs onto the plate on the 588ms spring. Print and reduced motion: still. With no WebGL, the band’s own gradient and grain stand, as in the app.
