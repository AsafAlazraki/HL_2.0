What only this one does

The photograph is the page. It runs off the top and both sides, takes 68% of the SP560’s sheet and 53% of the Stacer’s, and every word stands on its water and on the night it dissolves into. No paper shows. It is the only cover that prints a ground, and the only one that answers the critic’s “a letterhead with a framed picture” with the picture itself.

The rule: the photograph spans the sheet’s width at the tallest size that keeps the whole boat with air on both ends and never draws one held pixel wider than one CSS pixel. The name stands 16px under the boat’s lowest point, never on the hull, and the water melts into the night from 40px above that point. Today’s cover draws the picture at most 131 mm (495px) tall inside the margins.

With a 1,024 px photograph

The bleed stops where the pixels stop: the 529’s 1,024 × 676 is drawn at one to one, 794 × 676 (60% of the page), cut 230px across, and the night takes the rest (the-1024-photograph.html). It prints at 96 dpi, the softest of the three. A full-page bleed would need it 1,701px wide: refused.

How it reflows

  • 390 × 844: the photograph is a band the phone’s width in the same crop (390 × 373 for the SP560), the name at 30px under the boat, the facts two by two, the total at 40px under who it is for.
  • 844 × 390 and up: the A4 sheet at 1:1, as the paper view draws it from 826px; the first screen is the photograph and the name.
  • The PDF on a phone at fit-width (0.49): the name 31px, the total 24px, the customer 13px.

What Northside replaces

The photograph is the gallery’s cover (2026-11-02). Choosing it would need two things a person sets once, looking at it: where the crop sits and the boat’s lowest point. The ground is the night ink, not the accent, so any accent leaves it unchanged. A needs a scene. A studio render, a motor on white or no picture has nothing to dissolve, so that cover is the plate the paper prints today.

Motion, keys, reduced motion

On screen the build’s photograph morphs into the top of the sheet on the kit’s 588ms spring and grows to the bleed; the type does not move. Print and reduced motion: the sheet is simply there. A cover has no controls; it reads in DOM order: mark, reference, name, facts, who, total.

Honest constraints

  • A bleed reverses a recorded ruling (src/screens/document/art.ts: “a band and never a bleed”, measured at 300 dpi). By the spec’s own rule 6, CSS px, nothing is enlarged here, and the SP560 prints at 216 dpi against the 149 the band already accepts. Picking A needs a dated decision naming what lost.
  • An office printer cannot print to the edge: the PDF bleeds, the paper gets a white frame of a few millimetres on three sides. Every word is 18mm inside. The dark ground takes a lot of toner.
  • The SP560 photograph is the machine’s choice (person: false in pictures.json), so the paper’s reader (forCover) refuses it today. A person marks it once and it prints. It carries a Mercury 115, hence the caption, from the ledger’s own differs.
  • R. Kelleher is the name the walks type (e2e/mint.ts). Both quotes and their figures are the given PDFs in docs/directions/paper-quote/. No validity date and no address line print: Settings holds no days and no address. The note beside the paper says so.
