The house rules — a document with a margin

Northside’s rules read as one page it could print and sign: numbered clauses under what they govern, with what each reaches and what the price list does unasked kept out in the margin, where the machine never writes into the text.

What only this screen does

It writes a rule the build obeys. Here that rule is a clause of a document: 2.4 is typed at a cursor at the end of the section it governs, and from the next pick the build refuses the Yamaha F25SWTC on the Highfield Classic 340 with the clause’s own because.

The five hard questions

  1. 1Writing. One sentence at 26px, its seven choices dotted-underlined in the file’s ink; a pressed word opens in place at body size, the current one ticked (here: the verb).
  2. 2Reach. Counted before Add, in the margin beside the clause: 61 of 289 boats, 45 of 540 offers taken, 171 of 241 motors refused, and the 32 ePropulsion it cannot read said out loud.
  3. 3Written against noticed. Said rules are text; noticed ones are boxed questions in the outer margin with numbered answers, and enter the text only on a yes.
  4. 4History. Not drawn: the build keeps none (fired is returned and dropped). The margin figure is today’s reading.
  5. 5Off. A word of the clause: “is off — kept here in full”. The clause keeps its number and its ink; never struck, never red.

Its silhouette against the built screens

This board: one landscape sheet the width of the room, cut by the bottom edge; a hanging column of section names, a text column, a ruled margin.

Rules as built: a white plate, four figures, then rounded bars with a bar chart at the right. A has no plate, no bars, no census row.

Document: an A4 portrait sheet at true size with crop marks. A is landscape, never a page box, and does not end.

Customers: a small card on paper with acts beside it and a dark yard band. A’s paper is the whole page and its margin holds figures, not acts.

Motion, as choreography

Pressing a word: its list rises 6px from the word’s baseline over --duration-open 200ms; the sheet does not move. Each choice recounts the margin’s figures through the kit’s Figure, 294ms. Add: the “being written” tag cross-fades to the clause’s source, the black edge thins to nothing over 441ms, and the ghost line for the next rule appears under it. Answering a margin question with 1: the box slides 16px into the text column and becomes 2.5 with its words unchanged, 441ms.

Keyboard and reduced motion

Every underlined word is a combobox in reading order, then the because, then Add; a focused margin box takes 1 and 2. Reduced motion: lists and boxes appear and figures change in place, with no rise and no slide.

Honest constraints

Northside has written 0 rules and no quote stands, so 2.4 is a draft drawn mid-sentence (the walk’s own because) and nothing is drawn as filed or as switched off. 16 workbook rules exist; the 10 that pair a boat with what goes on it are drawn, 7 said and 3 noticed; the other two noticed (F16, F12) cannot be said by a sentence yet, so their boxes will only offer “Leave it noticed”. 39 more patterns read off 53 tables and 15,691 rows wait behind the last section.
