What only this screen does

The five hard questions

  1. 1Writing. The sentence stays a sentence at 22px; its words are the same dotted controls, and every choice relights the field.
  2. 2Reach. The whole answer: 61 of 289 boats lit, 171 of 241 motors struck, 45 of 540 offers taken, and 32 dashed marks for the motors it cannot read, said as “a blank never refuses”.
  3. 3Written against noticed. Only one rule is on the field at a time; a noticed rule is laid on it from a dashed chooser and is never mixed with yours.
  4. 4History. Not drawn: no firing is kept. The field is today’s reading of the file, said as “counted now”.
  5. 5Off. Off is the verb word; the field stays drawn with its marks outlined instead of filled, under “Switched on, it would…”.

Its silhouette against the built screens

This board: a short sentence, then one wide plate of small squares in maker rows, then a census between hairlines.

Fitment: a boat’s render with numbered callouts and one horsepower axis of dots. B has no picture and no axis: marks are models in the file’s order.

Motion, as choreography

Choosing a word relights the field in reading order, maker by maker: each mark fades and scales from 0.85 to 1 with a 4ms stagger, the whole wave capped at 294ms; the plate and the labels never move. A refused motor’s strike draws corner to corner in 150ms. The census rolls through the kit’s Figure. Add: the marks stay, the chooser’s first line becomes “Yours · 1”.

Keyboard and reduced motion

The sentence’s words, then the chooser, then the field as one tab stop: arrows walk mark to mark and each says itself (“Highfield Classic 340, loses the Yamaha F25SWTC”). Reduced motion: the field changes state at once, no wave and no strike drawing.

Honest constraints

Every mark is a model or motor on sale, counted as pairReach counts (289 models, 241 motors), in the file’s own order; the five Classics are the five pairReach names. The sentence is a draft with the walk’s own because; nothing is filed. A rule about a motor and a kit (F16) or a pre-delivery kit (F12) cannot be laid on this field, because its marks would be kits; the chooser offers only what a sentence can say. No photograph, no history.
