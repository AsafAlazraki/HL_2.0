The spine — the rules in the order of who said them

One path read top to bottom from “A boat is picked” to “Everything else your price list pairs is offered”: the price list’s rules, then Northside’s, then what the file does that nobody wrote, so why a motor is or is not offered is a route, and a rule switched off is a node the path visibly goes round.

What only this screen does

It writes a rule the build obeys, at the place on the path where it acts: a + between two nodes opens a sentence there, and from the next pick the build refuses what it refuses, with its because. Off is drawn as the path going round a node that is still whole.

The five hard questions

  1. 1Writing. At a + on the spine, one sentence at 22px with dotted words; the state is a word of the line, “This rule [is off]”.
  2. 2Reach. Inside the node: B’s field shrunk to a strip a maker a line (289 marks), and three figures, all in the conditional while off.
  3. 3Written against noticed. Three edges: the file’s blue, Northside’s ink, the noticed dashed ochre (Linear’s “dashed means suggested”). Noticed is below Northside and above the fall-through: it can warn, never decide.
  4. 4History. Said once in the head: what a rule has refused on a quote is not recorded yet. A node gains a dated line when the build keeps one.
  5. 5Off. The node keeps full ink and its place; the spine bends round it with “The build passes it while it is off”. Nothing is grey, struck or red.

Its silhouette against the built screens

This board: a centred path with a capsule at each end, cards hung on it, a + on every joint, and a bend round the card that is off.

History: a spine at the far left edge with dated day nodes, and full-width rows of the day’s quotes. C’s spine is centred, undated, and joins sentences, not days.

Review: full-width plates stacked edge to edge, folding open by cost. C’s cards stand apart on a line, 880 wide, and never fold to a bar.

Rules as built: a plate, a census row, and three lists of bars by owner. C keeps the three owners but makes them one route with an end.

Motion, as choreography

Pressing a +: the joint opens and a blank node grows from the joint’s height to its own over 441ms; the nodes below slide down together; nothing above moves. Switching off: the state word swaps in place (150ms cross-fade) and the bend draws round the node’s left side top to bottom in 294ms; the node’s ink never changes. Switching on, the bend retracts the same way.

Keyboard and reduced motion

Reading order is the route: each node, then the + under it (“Write a rule here”), then the next; the state word is a combobox. Reduced motion: nodes appear in place and the bend is drawn at once.

Honest constraints

The order is by who said it, not an evaluation order: every rule in force is read on every pick, and where two refuse, the higher priority then the older speaks. The node drawn off is a draft (Northside has 0 rules, no quote stands); its words and every figure are pairReach’s and costSaid’s for that sentence. Only the first noticed rule can be said as yours; the other two need a sentence about kits.
