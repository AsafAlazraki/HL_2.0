The boat's own bar

A boat on Home is pressed like a model on a maker's site: it opens the boat's own page, and Quote it rides on the card's corner. On that page a bar stays under the nav, carrying the boat's name, its places as tabs (Overview · Motor · Trailer · Fit-out) and Quote it with the running figure. Each tab answers one place in full, why-not included. Changing Northside's offer is a second, quieter button at the bar's end, and it turns the bar amber.

The one job
From one boat, see what goes on it and why, and quote it with what fits.
What only this screen does
A boat is the frame of the page: every place it has, and the quote, sit in one bar that never scrolls away. It reads like a maker's model page, except that every figure is from Northside's price file.

How it reflows at six sizes

  • 1920 and 1440: the bar is one line of name, tabs centred and two acts. Overview is the photograph at 60% beside the spec list, and Motor is five columns.
  • 1280 × 800: the same at a 1280 column, with the photograph shortened to 16:10 so the tiles start inside the fold.
  • 834 × 1112: the bar takes two lines, the name and Quote it, then the tabs. Overview stacks the photograph over the spec list, and Fit-out runs two cards across.
  • 844 × 390: the bar is one 52px line: the name, a Motor ▾ select and Quote it. "Change Northside's offer" moves to the end of each tab. The bar is the only chrome above the content.
  • 390 × 844: as at 844 × 390, with the select under the name. Motor becomes one card per motor and Fit-out a single column.

Type, motion, keyboard

Tile kicker · 11px caps Bar name · 22px regular Tab · 14px, the chosen one 600 with a 2px accent rule Reasons · 13px with the count at 20px light

Motion. The bar slides down from under the nav when the page head scrolls away (Apple's local nav), in 200ms. A tab change swaps the panel with no slide. Reduced motion: the bar is simply pinned.

Keyboard. The tabs are a tablist (arrow keys), and each tab is a URL param. Quote it is the bar's last stop, and the corner pill on a card is its own button.

Honest constraints, and what a dealer replaces

  • No Stabicraft PA 600 exists. The Patrol 600 is drawn and named as the near miss.
  • Dealer-fit packages show "No price set yet"; the rigging kit is outside every total and says so.
  • "Named for it, not paired" is counted from names, never offered on a quote.
  • No cost and no margin anywhere.
  • A dealer replaces the photograph (Overview closes to the spec list), the accent and the act's amber.

Against the simplest

It is more machinery than A: tabs, a sticky bar, a select on the phone, and a corner pill on every Home card that a salesperson must notice. It reads like a car maker's site, which customers know. But a person must press a tab to see the trailer, which is one press more than A's single scroll.
