Menu and receipt

What fits is a menu, and the quote is its receipt. Pressing a boat on Home slides out a drawer over Home, with what goes on the boat as choices and the quote those choices make pinned at its foot, so one press gives both. The drawer's last line opens the full page, which uses the same two panes at full width and adds why the rest do not go on it. In change mode the receipt pane becomes Northside's ledger of changes.

The one job
From one boat, see what goes on it and why, and quote it with what fits.
What only this screen does
What fits and the quote are one act. Every choice on the menu is a line on the receipt the moment it is made, so a salesperson builds the quote by reading what fits.

How it reflows at six sizes

  • 1920 × 1080: the drawer is 500px over Home's right side. The full page is a 1320px column, the menu at about 900px and the receipt at 380px.
  • 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800: as drawn. At 1280 the drawer narrows to 460px.
  • 834 × 1112: the drawer is 440px, with Home's cards two across beside it. On the full page the receipt drops under the menu as a bar pinned to the window's foot.
  • 844 × 390: the drawer is full-screen. The receipt is the one-line bar, which rises as a sheet capped at 80% of the height so the menu stays visible above it.
  • 390 × 844: the drawer is full-screen. The receipt bar sits over the nav and rises as a sheet (frame 4).

Type, motion, keyboard

Page title · 40px light Place · 22px semibold Choice · 14–16px, price tabular at the right Receipt line · 13px; total 26px semibold

Motion. The drawer slides in from the right over 260ms while Home dims, and Escape slides it out. A ticked line's row appears on the receipt over 120ms. The phone sheet rises over 240ms. Reduced motion: the drawer and sheet appear without sliding and the line is simply there.

Keyboard. The drawer is a dialog: focus enters at its title, Tab moves through the choices and on to Start the quote, and Escape closes it. Each place is a radio group or a set of checkboxes.

Honest constraints, and what a dealer replaces

  • No Stabicraft PA 600 exists. The Patrol 600 is the named near miss.
  • Dealer-fit packages and the rigging kit have no price set, and the receipt says so line by line.
  • "Named for it, not paired" is counted from names and never goes on the receipt.
  • No cost and no margin anywhere.
  • A dealer replaces the photograph (the drawer's head closes to text without it), the accent and the act's amber.

Against the simplest

It is the strongest picture of "choose, and see the quote", but it builds two surfaces for one boat: a drawer on Home and a full page. It also comes close to a second configurator beside the build at /quote/$id, which does the choosing again. That is the duplication A avoids.
