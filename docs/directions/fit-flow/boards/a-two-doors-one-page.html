Two doors, one page

Every boat on Home carries two doors side by side, What fits and Quote it. What fits opens one page per boat that reads from top to bottom: what it takes, its motor, its trailer, its fit-out, why the rest do not go on it, and a bar that always shows the quote those choices start. Northside's own changes happen in a separate mode on that same page, never beside a quote choice.

The simplest of the three: no drawer, no tabs, one scroll
The one job
From one boat, see what goes on it and why, and quote it with what fits.
What only this screen does
It is the one place a boat's whole offer is read and chosen: what goes on it, why the rest do not, and the quote it starts. Northside's changes to that offer are kept apart from it.

How it reflows at six sizes

  • 1920 × 1080: the 1200px column centred. The head's photograph is 420px wide and Home's eight cards sit in one row of eight.
  • 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800: as drawn. The capability row is seven figures across, and each place has its title column at 220px. At 1280 the column is 1200 less 80px of gutters.
  • 834 × 1112: the cards go four to a row and the capability row runs four across. A place's title sits above its list, and the reasons run two columns.
  • 844 × 390: the photograph shrinks to 240px beside the title, and its caption moves to the lightbox. The capability row runs four across. The quote bar is 56px, one line with the figure and the button.
  • 390 × 844: the photograph runs full bleed at the top, then the title, version and price. The capability row is a 2-by-4 grid, and each line puts its name over its facts. The quote bar is two lines above the phone's nav.

Type, motion, keyboard

Kicker · 11px caps, tracked 0.14em Title · 40px light, 30px on a phone Place · 22px semibold Line · 16px semibold name, figure tabular, right Facts and reasons · 13px, one measure of about 70 characters

Motion. Pressing What fits carries the card's photograph to the head, using the travel Home's tiles already make, in 240ms. Nothing else moves. Choosing a motor changes the bar's figure in place. A changed row's tag fades in over 120ms. Reduced motion: no travel, and the tag is simply there.

Keyboard. Each card is two buttons in tab order. The motor list is one radio group (arrow keys) and the fit-out is checkboxes. The bar's button is the last stop, and the jump chips are links.

Honest constraints

  • There is no Stabicraft PA 600 on the price file. The board draws the Highfield Patrol 600 that the name points at, and says so in Find.
  • Dealer-fit packages have no chosen price, so they read "no price yet", never $0.
  • The rigging kit's price is not set, so every total says "before the rigging kit".
  • "Named for it, not paired" counts names only. It never claims a fit and never offers the line on a quote until Northside pairs it.
  • The Sport 560 photograph shows a Mercury 115 and a finish no SP560 row carries; its caption says so.
  • No cost and no margin anywhere; prices are at Cash, the level the page names.

What a dealer can replace

The photographs (with no photograph, the head closes to the title and the capability row), the act's colour (the amber of Quote it and Start the quote), the accent, and which eight boats Home shows (Arrange Home). The two doors stay two doors.

Why it is the simplest

One new control on Home, a second door per card. One page per boat with nothing hidden behind a press. One bar for the quote, and one mode for Northside's changes. It uses no drawer, no tabs and no second copy of the page.
