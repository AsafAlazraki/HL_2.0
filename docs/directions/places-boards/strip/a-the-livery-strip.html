A · The livery

Every place is a card on its own ground, its mark in white at the size of a name, grouped by what it sells; opened, the card becomes the page and keeps its colour, mark and picture.

Drawn from live2/f1-teams.png (each team a card on its own colour, the car as the picture) and hand/f1-team-mclaren-390.png (opened, the livery stays and the sections begin). Neither is any built screen's reference.

The mark, as showpiece

Where held, the white ink stands on the place's own ground at 40–48 px, in a run where every mark has the same height. Stabicraft's white-only mark shows here for the first time: Data and Home can only set its name.

A dark-only mark (Formosa, Surtees, Haines, Yamaha, Mackay, GFAB) sits on an inset white plate. REDCO / Tinka and NSM Custom have no mark, so their names are set as a wordmark in the display weight. No initials, no stand-in.

The hero, never a lie

A card shows a photograph only from the ledger's hero tier for its own table, and the caption always prints the model it shows ("Highfield ADV7 on the water").

Until Northside chooses, the ledger's first one is shown. With none held (Jeanneau, every motor, trailer and list), the colour field stays and nothing is put in its place.

The line

Each place carries Northside's own one-line description, written in its settings ("What is Stacer to Northside, in a line?"). On day one every line is empty, so no card prints one, and nothing is guessed from the name.

Counts are moduleCensus's, the reader card and page share: live rows, with Dealer Fit's 201 and Parts' 699 held back said on the page.

How it reflows

390: one column; the picture goes on top at full width and the mark sits under it on the ground, never over the photograph. Kinds become a scrolling chip row. 834: two across, small cards three across. 1920: boats five across, the counted cell closing the run; small cards stay 178 px so a run never stretches a mark.

What Northside changes

  • Each place's ground, from the six kind inks and its accent. A white mark refuses a ground it cannot hold at 4.5 : 1, with that sentence, where it was chosen.
  • The mark: uploaded, pasted by address, or the ledger's own.
  • The picture, from its own range only.
  • The line, and the order by drag.

With no picture at all it still holds: Jeanneau's card is the proof.

Not one shape twice

Data sets seven makers as white plates with dark marks and their pairings as lines, over a ledger: the register of the file. Home sets the same seven as white plates and its boats as photographs on white cards.

A is the only screen of saturated coloured grounds: 25 cards in kind runs, marks in white, the picture fading into the colour. Nothing else in the app is a wall of colour.
