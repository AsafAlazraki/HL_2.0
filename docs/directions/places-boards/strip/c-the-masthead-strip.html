The mark is the page's title, set at 150 px on white where the h1 would be, with the range as a family strip of series under it and the picture as something Northside chooses from its own photographs, never a default.

The mark, as showpiece

The mark replaces the heading. At 150 px tall it is the largest thing on the page, half as tall again as Northside's own on Home; nothing competes with it, because the page is white and the picture sits lower.

Stabicraft's only mark is white, so its masthead turns to the night ground (neutral-950) and the rest of its page stays day. REDCO / Tinka and NSM Custom are set as a wordmark at display size (see Registration Costs beside), with "Upload theirs, or paste its address" in Settings.

The hero, chosen, never defaulted

C draws the honest day one: no picture is shown until Northside picks one. The panel offers exactly the ledger's hero photographs of this table (11 for Stacer), each captioned with its model, and upload or paste.

The ledger's licence note is said once under the offer, svgl's way. Once chosen, the picture takes the panel's place at its own proportions, so a 3 : 2 photograph is never cut to a letterbox that loses the boat.

The line

The line sits beside the mark at 16 px, the only text at the masthead. Empty on day one: a dashed prompt that asks the question ("What is Stacer to Northside, in a line?") and says nothing prints until it is written. Written, it is the page's subtitle.

Series with no picture show their own model numbers in mono: real figures from the list, not a stand-in.

Not one shape twice

Home centres Northside's mark on a 1,360 × 457 band of water. Data puts seven marks small on white plates. The picker's rail lists one boat's rows to choose a price.

C has no band, no plate and no price: one maker's mark set loose on white as the page's title, then a family strip of series as doors. No built screen sets a maker's mark as its heading.
