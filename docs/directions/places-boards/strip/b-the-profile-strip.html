B · The profile

A place is a profile: its mark in a wide tile beside its name, a line, counted facts and tabs that carry their counts, with what it pairs with as marks in the rail, so the join tables live inside the place they belong to.

Drawn from live/github-org-vercel.png (the mark tile, the grey line, tabs with counts, people in the rail) and live/github-org-microsoft.png (the organisation's picture framed inside the overview, not as the page's ground). Neither is any built screen's reference.

The mark, as showpiece

The mark gets a tile shaped like itself, 272 × 108, at the head of its own page, never a square that shrinks a wide mark to a stamp. It is the first thing on the page and the one object the eye lands on.

Stabicraft's white-only mark takes a night tile (neutral-950), drawn in the index beside. A place with no mark (REDCO / Tinka, NSM Custom) has its name set in the tile, and Settings says: "No mark held for REDCO / Tinka. Upload theirs, or paste its address."

The hero, never a lie

The picture is a framed banner inside the overview, not the page's ground, so a place with none simply has no banner and the page still reads (Jeanneau, every motor and list).

The caption always names the model and the host it came from. "Choose the picture" offers only hero-tier ledger photographs of this table, then upload or paste, recorded as Northside's own.

The line

One grey line under the name, as GitHub sets an organisation's. Empty on day one, it shows as a dashed prompt only Northside's staff see ("Northside's line for Highfield: not written yet, so nothing prints"). Written, it is plain ink, and it is what the index row and the quote's place line print.

How it reflows

What Northside changes

  • The mark in the tile, and the tile's ground for a white-only mark.
  • The banner picture, from this place's own range, or its own upload.
  • The line.
  • Its accent moves the tab underline and every link.

Without a picture the overview starts at the range; nothing collapses.

Not one shape twice

Customers rests on one person drawn on a paper letter; Data has no head object at all, only plates and a ledger; Home leads with a band.

B is the only page with an object's identity at its head: a mark tile beside a name, a ruled tab row with counts, an 840 main and a 340 rail of partners. No built screen carries that silhouette.
