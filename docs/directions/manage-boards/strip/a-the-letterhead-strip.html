The letterhead

The quote’s own paper is the form: every line a customer will read is pressed where it prints, and a line nobody has typed visibly prints nothing.

Order and grouping

In print order. The head of page 1 (mark, name, ABN, street, phone, website), then Prepared by and Valid until, then the last page (terms, deposit, GST). People, the look, Home and every other setting fold into four bands at the foot, each saying its state in a sentence.

What only this screen does

It puts each of Northside’s details where the customer will read it, so the manager edits the quote, not a form about the quote.

Home, Northside’s to arrange

Home’s band opens to its four sections, each with an eye and a handle, and Home itself carries an Arrange press at the sea’s foot that does the same on the thing.

What Northside can change about it

Its mark (dark ink on the paper, its light ink shown beside for the water), its trading and legal name, every contact line, its own terms and deposit wording, its GST rate and days of validity, its people, and its colour, which prints as “Quotation”. The paper itself stays white: an office printer frames a tint in white.

How its silhouette differs from the built screens

Document (/quote/$id/document, built) is a tall A4 sheet centred on the floor with a “Not printed” column at its right and a Print act. This is the head of a page cropped wide, hairline leaders fanning to both margins, and four folded bands at its foot: no page count, no print act, no column. Prices and Data are registers of rows; nothing here is a row.

Motion, keyboard, reduced motion

A press on a printed line opens its field in the margin: the field slides 8 px from the edge over --duration-enter (200 ms, --ease-out) and the leader draws from the line to the field in the same 200 ms. The page never moves or rescales. Keep changes the line in place, and one toast offers Undo. Reduced motion: the field and leader appear at once.

Tab walks the printed lines in reading order. Enter opens a line’s field, Esc closes it unkept, and Ctrl+Enter keeps.

Honest constraints

No draft exists on this computer, so the body of the page is a sentence. Nothing is pre-filled: docs/DECISIONS.md holds no yes to offering the original HelmLogic’s address and phone.
