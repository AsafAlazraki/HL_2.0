The shelf · C · The two shelves · the strip beneath the boardThis browser on the left, the file you brought on the right, a gutter between them that says for every line which way it moves, and one sentence across the top that says what pressing the act will do.

Order and grouping

Operation sentence → the two shelves → lines, grouped by table and then by which way a line moves (→ change, + new, = kept, ? can't place). Backup is the left shelf going out ("Save a copy of this shelf"); restore is a copy coming in on the right, through the same door as a price file. Start again hangs off the left shelf, because it empties this browser.

Apply with confidence (Q1)

Both figures are on screen at once and the winner is the right-hand side, said by the arrow, never by colour alone. A missing side is hatched and named ("not on this browser", "not in this file · kept exactly as it is"), so a dealer sees that 128 rows survive a file that forgot them.

Before and after Apply (Q2)

  • Before: the bar says "Undo puts all 3,655 back in one step" before the act, not after it, and "Change this browser · 3,655" names the side that moves.
  • After: the gutter turns to = on every line, the right shelf reads "Brought in today · Undo", and the bar becomes "Applied 3,655 · Undo".

Reflow

  • 390: the shelves shrink to two small cards with the swap between; the census is four tiles; lines become one card each with its glyph, "here → there".
  • 834: the panes narrow to 360 each and the gutter stays; names wrap to two lines.
  • 1920: the panes gain a supplier column and Sell inc Install; the bar stays 1,792 wide.

What Northside changes

The mark on the left shelf (its organisation row in marks-ledger.json, or a dealer upload), the level each table is read at, and the accent and act colours. The bar is ink, so it holds whatever accent Northside picks.

Silhouette against the built screens

No built screen is two equal panes with a gutter. The cascade is a picture on the left and grouped prices on the right; Review is one centred card; Fitment is one boat on its plate. The ink bar across the foot is this screen's alone; every other act in the app is an amber pill on light ground.

Type, motion, reach

Names 13, figures 13/600 left and 13/700 right, sentence 26/700. On Apply each gutter glyph turns to = in reading order, 24 ms apart, 200 ms each (--duration-enter); reduced motion swaps them at once. Tab walks the sentence, the shelves' acts, each fold, the bar; arrow keys move line by line.
