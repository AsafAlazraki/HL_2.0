What Northside changes from its own settings

  • Which fact rows show, and their order. Controls, Tilt, Weight and Colour are the motor list’s own columns; the rigging kit is the Highfield × Yamaha row’s. A fact the same on every column folds into “The same on all”.
  • The price level the head figure is printed at: Cash, as fitment reads today, or Trade.
  • Its own rules. A motor one of Northside’s rules refuses keeps its column, struck in its head, with the rule’s own because under the name.

Its silhouette, against what is built

  • Prices runs fourteen lists across over rungs down, on a dark foot of costs. D has no rungs and no money ground: its columns are one boat’s options, headed by a model at 22 px, one of them tinted.
  • The sheet is the price list’s own grid of lines. D’s rows are facts and its columns things, with a sum under it.
  • Fitment as built centres a 600 px render with callouts. D’s only picture is a 150 px thumbnail.

The five hard questions, answered here

  1. By what is left: the seven made for it are the page; the 202 others never stand as a list at rest, only as five counted terms.
  2. Struck rows appear in a note’s first or nearest example and in Show all: the name in quiet ink under a hairline, the engine’s reason said once on the note above it. Nothing is red.
  3. The toolbar is the printed sum. The pressed term is the view, every term is counted, and the equals sign says what is on sale. Show all 209 is always last.
  4. To the price list and back: each column opens its Highfield × Yamaha row; each note opens the deciding cell (the boat’s Min HP or Max HP). Back returns to /fitment?boat=…&slot=motor.
  5. The limits as facts: a 150–200 band under every horsepower with the motor pinned in it; in the trailer view, ATM is a row: “Rated 1,990 kg · the boat weighs 740 kg · clears it by 1,250 kg”.
