B · The envelope

The boat’s limits are places you can point at beside the buyer — 150–200 hp is a lit band, 740 kg is a line — and every motor and trailer Northside sells is a mark at its own figure.

How it reflows

The axes turn vertical: each horsepower is a row and its motors a line of dots, the band a lit stripe of three rows. The trailer lanes stay horizontal at 250px; the paired trailer is said under them in one line.

The hard questions, answered here

  1. Grouped by measured value: nothing is listed at rest — 209 motors and 410 trailers are marks, in lanes by standing.
  2. Struck: a grey mark in its lane; inspected, its card says the reason in a quoted box and names the deciding cell.
  3. The toolbar: the foot line — a pressed chip, 7 of 209, the narrowing named, 202 hidden but still on the axis, Show all.
  4. To the sheet: the card’s “Open Highfield × Yamaha at row 863”; Back returns with the same mark inspected.
  5. The limits: 150–200 hp is a lit band with the motors standing in it; 740 kg is a line the trailers stand either side of.

Its silhouette, against what is built

This board: two wide instrument plates, a lit vertical band and a vertical line, and dots — no rows at rest. Nothing built is a plot.

Review C (this round): the file drawn as horizontal bars per table with marks on them. B’s marks are motors and trailers at a measured figure, against one boat’s two limits.

The configurator: a photograph and chapters of priced choices. B never shows a picture and never a price list; it shows where things sit.

History: a vertical spine of days. B’s only lines are the band and the load, and they are limits, not time.

Data: plates and a register. B’s plates hold axes, not rows.

Owed by the engine: a reader in src/domain/fitment that splits Max HP into total, count and per-engine (FITMENT_RULES F1), with a test — until it exists the 47 rigs sit in their tray and the band is true of single motors only. Honest today: the engine does not yet produce outside-envelope; the band is the row’s own Min HP and Max HP, drawn as a fact, and the pairing still decides.
