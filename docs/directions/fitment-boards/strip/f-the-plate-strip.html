F · The plate

Grouped by the cell that decides. The boat’s own row is cast as a plate: its horsepower, its weight, its trailer, and what is made for it. Press a line and it shows what that line lets on and what it holds back, each group headed by the very price-list cell that decides it and opening onto it.

What Northside changes from its own settings

  • The plate’s lines. Motor, weight, trailer and made-for-it by default; dealer fit and parts can be added.
  • The plate’s colour. It reads the panel token: navy by default, or Northside’s own colour once its appearance is set.
  • The render, shown in its window or left out. A boat with none shows no window and no stand-in.
  • Which line opens on arrival.

Its silhouette, against what is built

  • Prices has a dark band too: a full-width foot of costs under a ladder. F’s dark is one centred object at the top, holding the boat’s name, with rivets and an inset rule, and no money on it.
  • The picker’s three-fact strip sits under a photograph. F’s facts are four lines you can press, each with its consequence.
  • Review stacks plates by money. Fitment as built centres a 600 px render; F’s render is 236 px, in the plate’s corner.

The five hard questions, answered here

  1. By reason, named by its cell: Max HP · 200 HP holds back 51, Min HP · 150 HP 74, Highfield × Yamaha 30, and the one-motor figures 47.
  2. Struck rows show only as the nearest example under each cell and in Show these. The reason is the cell together with the engine’s sentence, said once.
  3. One line: Motor · 209 Yamahas on sale · [Made for it · 7] [Held back · 202] · Show all 209.
  4. To the price list and back: Open the cell takes you to the sheet at the boat’s row, with Max HP lit. Add one there opens Highfield × Yamaha. Back returns with the line still open.
  5. The limits are the plate: “150–200 hp, one motor”, and “740 kg — both trailers clear it”.
