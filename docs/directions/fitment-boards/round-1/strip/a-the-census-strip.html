Every trailer Northside sells is one square, so how much of the catalogue this boat takes is seen before a single row is read — and the standings themselves are the toolbar.

What Northside changes from its own settings

Which standings openThe default chips: Paired and Built for Highfield (F8’s 12 of 434) out of the box. A salesperson’s own press is remembered per browser.
What “no brand named” meansNot checked, by default. Northside can say fits or needs a look — one setting, every boat.
The load floorWhich column is the load (Boat Weight kg) and which the capacity (ATM). It rings a square ochre; it never removes one.
Its own rulesStruck here on the next reading, with their own because.

The hard questions, answered here

  1. 2,400 excluded: they never were. 434 trailers are 444 squares in five standings, and a reason is said once, on its marque’s block.
  2. Struck: the name in quiet ink under one hairline, the reason above it in full ink, the deciding cell as a link. No red, no warning glyph.
  3. The toolbar: the standings are the chips; the line after them names the narrowing, counts the 303 hidden and offers Show all 434.
  4. To the sheet: a row’s series banner opens /data/$table on that row; one Back returns here with the same chip pressed.
  5. The limits: “Takes 150–200 hp” and “Weighs 740 kg” in the head; a trailer rated under 740 kg is rung ochre, never removed.

Its silhouette, against what is built

This board: a full-width field of squares under one sentence, then a row of chips, then eight equal blocks. Nothing on the built app is a field of squares.

Data: plates across the top and one long register under them. A’s blocks are equal, in a 4 × 2 grid, and none of them is a table.

Quotes: bands that are states, in rows. A’s groups are standings drawn as quantities, and they run across the page, not down it.

The configurator: a photograph at the left and chapters at the right. A has no photograph beyond a 168px thumbnail, and no column.

Review D (this round): one row per check with four figures. A has no rows of figures: it has one sentence, and the squares are the figures.

Owed by the engine: one pure sayVerdict in src/domain, with a test, writes “Its series banner names Stacer, not Highfield.” No sentence on this board may be written in a component. Owed to the quote: one trailer per quote, decided over all trailer blocks — the star wins.
