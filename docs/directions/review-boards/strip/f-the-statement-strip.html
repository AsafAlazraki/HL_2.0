Review · the strip beneath board F

F · The settled statement — grouped by state, read like a bank statement

The price list is reconciled the way an account is: every check is one statement line with what it read and what is owed each way, the lines sit under the state they are in, nothing is ever netted, and a closing balance says what is still to settle.

The five questions, on this board

Readable, and acted on
Money in the columns a dealer already reads: Too little and Too much, right-aligned, subtotalled, with a balance under a double rule. An open account is something a business settles.
Grouped
By state: Costs money, Could give a wrong pick, Found nothing, Left as meant. Inside each, by what it costs. A check moves band when its state changes; its line never disappears.
Applied
A line opens into its items, each with its own small act (“Make it Large”). One item a press, one step through catalogue/commands.ts, Undo in the toast; the line’s figures and the balance re-add.
Dismissed
An item left as meant moves to the Left as meant band with its reason (It’s meant · The check is wrong here) and its value; it returns to its line when the value moves.
Clean day
See right: every line sits under Found nothing, ticked, and the balance reads $0 and $0.
