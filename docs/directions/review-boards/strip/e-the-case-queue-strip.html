E · The case queue — one case at a time, in what it costs

The screen holds one case: the fix as its title, the file’s own cells as the evidence, one priced act, a leave with two reasons, and what is kept said before the press — and the queue ends, so the last case settled is the clean day.

The five questions, on this board

Readable, and acted on
The title is the fix, not the fault: “Register the ALP 5.3M-13B as a Large trailer.” The act carries the money: “Make it Large · +$117”. There is nothing else on screen to close instead.
Grouped
By what it costs, one case per fix: the money first (9), then a wrong pick, then tidy. A case may clear several lines (“Price the 8 Lone Star winch kits”), and Tidy is one case at the very end.
Applied
One press, one batch through catalogue/commands.ts (Rego Type, Rego ($), Sell inc Rego together), said in a toast with Undo, kept in History; the next case slides up.
Dismissed
“Leave it” takes It’s meant or The check is wrong here, keyed on the line and the value that made it a case, so it returns when the value moves. Skip keeps its place.
Clean day
See right. The queue runs out, and its last screen is the settled account.
