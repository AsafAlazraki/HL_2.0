Shut to the model

The sheet rests one line per boat: 67 lines under seven series, each carrying in its own columns what every variant shares and a drawing of its price — PVC against HYP on the series’ own scale — and a press opens its variants in place.

The seven principles, answered

  1. Said once. saidOnce lifts table-wide facts to the head sentence, series facts to the series line (Roll-Up: Tiller · S shaft · External Tank · 3 air chambers · colourways WH · LG), and a model’s shared facts become the model line itself: its cells ARE the band’s constants. Colourways are said on the series line where every model shares the set (Roll-Up, Ultralite, Classic); in Sport 15 of 16 share and SP660 adds DG-G-WB, so there the drawer says them.
  2. Columns a dealer reads. Model, Length, Beam, People, HP (min–max), the Cash figure per material, the count. Codes, colourways and the three figures per row are in the drawer; the rest in the record; “Every row 588” is the full register.
  3. Picture — not in the grid. No row here is tall enough to draw a boat, so none tries. Pictures (35 of 67 models) draws each held render at 320 px; the record draws the variant’s own.
  4. Rest is the work. 67 lines; no panel; no form. Adding a column lives in the Every row door’s column menu.
  5. Eighteen. 19 lines in view and a capacity of 19 (right) — and a line here is a boat, 4 to 16 variants each. The route must name .bm-lines and the critic should rule whether a model line is a record; the ruler’s own definition (a row of more than one cell) says it is.
  6. Not /quotes. A single full-width ledger; the record rises from the foot as a sheet 40 % tall over the lines, never a column beside them.
  7. Every function kept. A model-line cell edits the band: Beam on RU280KAM writes its 4 rows as one batch, counted before it lands, with Undo; a Coaster cell whose rows differ reads “varies · 7” and opens the drawer instead. In the drawer every cell edits one row, refusal in the cell. Mod D down model lines fills their bands. Doors Models · Pictures · the record. Keys on the lit line (“Enter opens 4”), none at 390. A pairing table rests one line per hull (588 hulls on Highfield × Yamaha; the SP560 HYP B-B-B reads “4 motors · F90XB ★”), its motors in the drawer. Cost only in the Every row door, said at the head.

Against /quotes

Stripes to both edges, a green drawing in every line, no side element at all. At rest there is nothing to the right of the list but the list.

The critique’s eight

  • (22) columns. From 33 columns in a sideways-scrolling grid to four shared facts and one drawn figure per boat. Matrix is said once in the head; Image Link lives in Pictures; Variant lives in the drawer as Colourway + Material.
  • (11) the form. Gone; the column menu is behind Every row.
  • (1) ?at=. ?at=boat_highfield:496 opens the SP560 drawer, lights HBS126 (HYP B-B-B) a third of the way down the room and raises its record from the foot; the address keeps the id. The flow asserts the lit code.
  • (12) folds. Which drawers are open is ?open=SP560,… in the address, and a write never touches the address — so no write can shut one. Tested: open, edit, still open.
  • (5) density. 19 lines at 1280 × 800.
  • (6) the shared shape. No list-and-panel: the record comes up from the foot.
  • (13) ways back. No Data or Home on the screen; the pill’s lit door; “Data” once.
  • (20) 390. The sentence sits above the list; the list pads by --shell-foot.

How it reflows

  • 1920: the price track grows to 420 px and the Trade figure joins as a second drawing on the same line.
  • 834: Beam goes to the record; the track is 160 px.
  • 390: a line becomes two: the name and its count; its shared facts and the figure per material. A press opens the drawer full-width.

What a second dealership replaces

--color-ground, --color-accent, and the inks, which are their file’s own section accents (Motor Envelope carmine, Hull Only Pricing viridian on this one). B needs no picture, so a dealership whose file holds none sees exactly this. On a table with one row per model (Stacer) a line is the row itself and nothing opens.

The engine: what stays, what is written

Stays: the fold — a shut drawer takes its cells out of the addressable set, so at rest the grid addresses 67 lines × 5 cells and navigation, paste and fill are unchanged. Written, pure and tested: saidOnce; materialFigures(model, rung), the per-material figures behind the drawing (asserts RU230KAM: PVC $2,770, HYP $4,500–$5,320); bandWrite(node, field, value), one updateCell per row in one batch. At rest 67 + 7 lines are in the DOM, under VIRTUALIZE_ABOVE, so nothing is windowed.

Motion, keys, reduced motion

A drawer opens as a height change in 100 ms, --duration-press; the record rises from the foot in 300 ms on --ease-drawer — three times the RAIL figure, said here rather than hidden — its words present from the first frame, and the lit line stays where it is. Reduced motion: both appear without travel. Keys: ↑ ↓ or J K lines, Enter opens and shuts a drawer, → steps into its first row, Space the record, Mod D fill, / find — printed on the lit line, never at 390.
