The price list

The sheet is printed like the maker’s own price list: each model is one block whose spine says once what its variants share and carries its held render, and its rows say only what differs — colourway, material, the code, the three figures.

recommended

The seven principles, answered

  1. Said once. A new pure saidOnce(rows, fields, levels) lifts what is constant: across all 588 rows — Matrix, Base Freight $0 cost, Other Charges $0 cost — to the head; across a series (Roll-Up: Tiller · S shaft · External Tank · 3 air chambers) to its band; across a model to its spine. 66 of 67 models hold one value in each of 16 spec columns; Coaster, whose 7 rows are different hulls, keeps 11 of them per row. Over the whole pack it finds 104 columns in 41 of 53 tables that say one thing, and 10 that are empty on every row.
  2. Columns a dealer reads. Colourway and Material (the file’s own Variant, read as its description says, “material × colourway” — 556 of 588 split; the other 32 print as typed), Model code, Cash (the rung, lit), Trade, Warranty. “11 more ▸” holds the rest, three of them marked cost; “Every column 33” is a door.
  3. Picture. In the spine, the model’s first held render at 164 × 92 from a 1100 × 619 copy — never enlarged. 35 of 67 models hold one; the band says “pictures held for 1 of 8” once instead of 242 cells saying “held as a link”. No picture column.
  4. Rest is the work. No panel and no form. “Add a column” is the last line of the “11 more ▸” menu: a sentence naming the table and the 588 rows it would land empty on, the act under it, refusing nothing until pressed.
  5. Eighteen. 20 records in view and a capacity of 20 at the file’s first screen (right).
  6. Not /quotes. No column is reserved to the right at any width; the one side element is the picture spine on the LEFT of its own rows, and it scrolls with them.
  7. Every function kept. Each value a real button; Enter edits, the engine’s refusal is a pill under the cell; Mod D fills and paste lands over the same GridModel — the spine is a rowheader, not addressable, so navigation, paste and fill never learn it exists; Undo pinned in the chapter row after a write; doors Price list · Pictures · the record (Space, opened under its row); keys printed on the lit row and nowhere at 390; a pairing table’s spine is the hull, its rows the motors, ★ recommended first; cost said at the head of every cost column.

Against /quotes

One paper sheet, a picture spine down the left edge, no panel. The built sheet was list-left, 360 px detail-right at --spacing(90), the same token as /quotes and /data’s plate.

The critique’s eight

  • (22) columns. Five of 33 columns in 990 px, three saying nothing → six that differ, beside a spine. Matrix is said once in the head; Image Link becomes the spine render or the band’s count; Variant becomes Colourway + Material.
  • (11) the form. Gone with the panel. Adding a column is a press on “11 more ▸”, and nothing refuses before it.
  • (1) ?at=. Read as a row id: the block scrolls to a third of the room, the row lights, its record opens beneath it. ?at=boat_highfield:496 lights HBS126, SP560 HYP B-B-B; an unknown id is a sentence where the row would be. The flow asserts the lit code, not only the address.
  • (12) folds. Nothing folds at rest: a series is a chapter, a position (?series=). A shut model block (Alt ↑) lives in ?shut= keyed by its group path, which a write cannot touch; the test shuts, edits, and asserts still shut.
  • (5) density. 20 of 18 at 1280 × 800, by the ruler’s own formula.
  • (6) the shared shape. No right column, no --spacing(90).
  • (13) ways back. The screen’s own Data and Home buttons are gone; the pill’s lit Data door is the way back and drops its “‹ Data” chip wherever its own door is lit (a shell change, to be dated in DECISIONS). “Data” is said once.
  • (20) 390. The sentence explaining the reduction sits above the list; the list pads by --shell-foot, so its last row clears the tab bar.

How it reflows

  • 1920: spine 480 with the render at 220 × 124; three more columns join (Tube, Int length, Registration).
  • 834: spine 300, render 120 × 68; Colourway, Material, Code, Cash. Trade and Warranty move to the record.
  • 390: the spine becomes each block’s head, render 128 × 72; rows of 44 px carry Colourway, Material, Cash; chapters scroll sideways, faded at the edge.

What a second dealership replaces

--color-paper (the sheet), --color-accent (spine edge, lit chapter), their makers’ marks from their own ledger, their renders. With no picture a spine is typography and keeps its height, because a spine is always as tall as its rows. The spine is whatever the innermost group is: on Parts a category (2,937 products under 187), on a pairing the hull, with the hull’s own render read through its link; on a table of one row per model (Stacer, 91) the block is one row, the render goes to the record and to Pictures, and the spine is the model’s name.

The engine: what stays, what is written

Stays: the GridModel over variant rows, grouping.ts, sections.ts, every command and its inverse. Written, pure and tested on the pack: saidOnce (asserts 3 table-wide, 4 on Roll-Up, 16 on 66 of 67 models), materialAndColourway (asserts 556 of 588), modelPicture (asserts 35 of 67). The renderer draws a model as one CSS grid with the spine spanning its rows; blocks are content-visibility: auto chunks from chunkLines, so all 588 rows stay in the DOM and in find-in-page, as the reference asks, instead of windowing past VIRTUALIZE_ABOVE 150.

Motion, keys, reduced motion

The record opens beneath its row as a height change in 100 ms, --duration-press on --ease-out — inside the RAIL figure the reference quotes; the rows below move down with it and nothing else moves; nothing moves while a caret is in a cell. Reduced motion: the record is simply there. Keys: J K or ↑ ↓ rows, ← → cells, Enter edits, Space the record, Mod D fill, Mod Z undo, / find, [ ] the chapter before and after — printed on the lit row, never at 390.
