## The verdict

**Mostly no, and the change of mind is now the best moment in the sale.**

- One press puts the F115LB on instead of the F90LB. The tile says what the press will do ("+$2,337 instead of the F90LB"). The toast says it as a person would, Ctrl Z takes it back, and the total moved in one frame every time.
- Giving the quote is a moment at every size: the card turns leaf green under a seal, and the photograph takes the leaf ring.

**Where he would still say it, first: Dealer fit, and the rigging kit the swap brought with it.**

- The swap puts on the F115LB's kit, and from then on the kit is named by the file's own description everywhere it appears: the shut head, the finale's note, the cascade and the customer's paper. That description is "Mech Rigging Kit · 704 Binnacle Mount w 6Y8 2 Gauges, 5 m Harness, Cables & Filter".
- Open Dealer fit and it is a settings list:
  - radio circles, each with a part code under it
  - "+$18 at Cash" five times down the right edge
  - two engine paragraphs per section
  - no glyph and no picture
- Under it, the customer's paper prints the kit as "Not priced on this quote" and the total says "One item is not priced on this quote and is not in this total".

**At the tablet, the boat is never big.** At 834 the boat is a 250 × 140 thumbnail in the masthead for the whole build.

## What is new

### Majors

**1. After the change of mind, the rigging kit is named by the file's description everywhere.** (configurator, cascade, document; 1440 / 834 / 390)

- **Before the swap,** Dealer fit's head read "Yamaha/Stacer · Binnacle Mount Rigging Kit · 6 more offered".
- **After the swap,** the kit is named by its file description in four places:
  - **the shut head:** "Mech Rigging Kit · 704 Binnacle Mount w 6Y8 2 Gauges, 5 m Harness, Cables & Filter · 6 more offered". That is three lines at 1440 and 834 and four at 390, with the dot hanging at the end of the second line.
  - **the finale's note:** "The Mech Rigging Kit · 704 Binnacle Mount w 6Y8 2 Gauges, 5 m Harness, Cables & Filter carries no price on this quote…"
  - **the cascade's held row**
  - **the customer's page 2**, under RIGGING KITS
- The swap is the moment the customer is watching, and what it leaves behind is the database's sentence.
- **Fix:** say the kit as a person would, for example "Rigging kit for the F115LB" or "Binnacle mount rigging kit", with the description under "For the workshop".

**2. Dealer fit is still a settings list.** (configurator, 1440 / 834 / 390)

- **Five parts are drawn as rows**, each with:
  - a radio circle
  - its code in mono under the name: 23661, 23211, 688-8258A-10, 110016
  - a "+$18" chip over "at Cash"
- **Each section opens with engine sentences:**
  - "5 OF 2,238 PAIRED WITH THIS HULL"
  - "Anything in Parts & Accessories can go on this quote. Whatever is not paired with this hull says so on its own line. 699 are no longer sold and are left out."
  - "The price file gives Rigging Kits no prices, so anything from it goes on this quote unpriced."
- **There is no glyph and no picture.** The motor chapter two heads up uses the kit's `OptionTile` with photographs. The kit's `Row`, `Stat`, `Chapter` and `Status` are used by no build surface.
- The second critique asked for "part rows with their kind's glyph and a name first". The toolbox was taken off and nothing replaced it.
- **Fix:**
  - Draw the five as kit option tiles, or as `Row` with the kind's glyph and the price as the only figure.
  - Drop "at Cash" where the quote is at Cash.
  - Move the paired counts and "no longer sold" behind "Show all".

**3. The pill's crest re-rises from a blur, and the shine runs again, every time a caret leaves a field.** (shell, seen on the build at 1440)

- The CSS promises the shine runs "once, as the business's mark lands, and never again on that page".
- **What was sampled every frame across one focus and one blur in the name field:**
  - **On focus:** `:root[data-still]` swaps `animation-name` to `way-crest-fades`, which restarts the animation. "NM" goes to opacity 0 and fades back over about 370 ms.
  - **On blur:** the name swaps back to `way-crest-arrives`, which restarts again. "NM" drops 6 px into a 4 px blur and rises over about 400 ms, then the shine sweeps the medallion for about 550 ms.
- **When it happens:** on every field the salesperson leaves, through the whole sale (the search, the name, the contact line).
- **Cause:** `src/screens/shell/shell.css:360–367`. Swapping `animation-name` under `[data-still]` replays a keyed one-shot animation.
- **Fix:** gate it with `animation-play-state`, or with a class set once when the mark lands, never by swapping the name.

**4. At the tablet, the boat is a thumbnail for the whole build.** (configurator, 834)

- **What it is:** at 834 the stage is a 250 × 140 photograph in the sticky masthead, from arrival to the give.
- **It never grows:**
  - not on arrival
  - not when a motor is chosen
  - not in the finale, which at 390 does carry a 311 px photograph
- **Why it matters:** "More like Porsche the right side, not less." At the size a salesperson holds on his knee, the boat is smaller than the motor tiles under it.
- **The thumbnail does answer the give** with its leaf ring.
- **Fix:** on arrival at 834, give the boat the full width above the specification, and let it shrink into the masthead band only as the page scrolls.

**5. The given paper tells the customer something is missing from the total.** (document, configurator finale)

- **What the customer reads,** under the total on page 1, on page 2's "Your price", and on the finale card:
  - "One item is not priced on this quote and is not in this total."
  - the kit's line itself: "Not priced on this quote"
- **What the file holds:** `rig_kits:216` (704-6Y82L-##-05) carries 2,210 / 2,099.50 / 2,099.50 in its sell columns. No price level is declared for Rigging Kits (DECISIONS 2026-09-25).
- **So the given quote is short,** and says so to the customer. Nowhere in the app can a level be declared, or a price put on the kit line.
- **This is decided, but it is the weakest sentence of the sale,** and the customer reads it at the moment of trust.
- **Fix:** offer "Put a price on this line" on an unpriced kit, as the hull's own "Put this price on the boat" does (DECISIONS 2026-09-24), or declare the level. Until then, say it to the dealer before the give, not to the customer after.
