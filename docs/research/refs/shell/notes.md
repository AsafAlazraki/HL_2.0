# The shell — the sweep

Driven 2026-09-22 in two runs: the first cut off by an account limit after capturing 115 frames into `live/`, `live2/`, `finder/` and `hand/`; this one read those ledgers, drove 10 more sources into `finder2/` and `hand2/` and wrote this. **135 sources · 122 frames on disk · 114 distinct images · 8 byte-identical pairs · 13 refused**, named in `sources-index.md`. **Every frame cited was opened and looked at** — 56 shell frames, four built screens and one stock frame through 43 Read calls. Paths are relative to `docs/research/refs/shell/`; stock is `C:\Users\Asaf\dev\hl-refs\ref\`.

**The job.** Twelve screens by the end of Milestone 2 and no way between them but the links each happens to draw. A dealer with a customer at the desk reaches any screen from any screen in one press, and a quote, customer, table or row by typing. Five built screens are Showroom on photography; the Cockpit registers owe 18 rows at 1280 × 800. Entry has no shell. A second dealership replaces the mark and the accent.

4. **Apple has folded navigation bars into Toolbars** — `finder2/apple-hig-navigation-bars.png` is byte-identical to `live/apple-hig-toolbars.png`. And the 2026 tab bar is a floating pill with search as a separate trailing bubble (`live2/apple-hig-tab-bars-ipad.png`) that on iPad sits at the TOP and converts to a sidebar on a button (`live2/apple-hig-tab-bars-ipad-2.png`).
5. **A top bar is the most expensive shell a register can have.** Read off `ref/porsche-top.png` at 1440 × 900: brand line, working row and gap put the stage about 200 px down — seven rows at the built register's 28 px pitch, where the register's own `Home` button sits in head height already paid (`docs/directions/quotes/built/after-1440-00-register.png`). A rail costs width, which a register has; a bar costs rows, which it owes.

## 1. What is genuinely best — the five hard questions

**1 · One object on a photograph and on a register?** Yes, in two materials. Material: "Always put the rail in the same place, even on different screens of an app" (`live/m3-navigation-rail.png`). Apple: over "bright, colorful content … prefer a monochromatic appearance", a badge only for "critical information" (`live2/apple-hig-tab-bars-ipad.png`). Highfield ships the showroom case from inside our own price file — a translucent dark band over full-bleed water, wordmark left, four words, `☰` (`live/highfield-nav.png`). Each built screen already carries a masthead with `NORTHSIDE MARINE` as an eyebrow top-left and its own acts top-right (`docs/directions/document/built/flow-1440-28-document.png`). The shell has one place to be on every screen, that eyebrow's line, and must not put a second head over it.

**2 · Where the mark is the showpiece without eating the work.** Porsche: the wordmark alone and centred with nothing at its height but `☰ Menu` far left and three glyphs far right, the working row — `←`, Save, the price, `Summary` — on its own line beneath (`ref/porsche-top.png`); at 390 it becomes the crest, still alone (`hand/porsche-model-start-390.png`). Every rail system demotes the mark to a switcher chip — `Linear ▾` (`live2/linear-app-shot-head.png`), and shadcn's header "for branding, titles, or workspace switchers" (`live2/shadcn-sidebar-scrolled.png`). The showpiece needs a line of its own, and the only shell with one is a bar, which §0.5 prices. The reconciliation: the mark large on Showroom screens where there is a head to spend, the crest on a register — one asset at two sizes (§6).

**3 · What the finder finds, and how five kinds sit in one list.** Spotlight names its kinds — apps, files, actions, the internet, the Clipboard — and draws them as round buttons beside the field (`live/apple-spotlight.png`). Vercel groups by kind with the verb as the row, `Projects › Search Projects… ⇧P · Create New Project…`, under a `Home` scope chip (`live/vercel-dashboard-shortcuts.png`). Ours: `Quotes › 20260918-01 SP560 · Marcus Webb · Open` · `Boats › SP560 · 15 rows · Start a quote` · `Tables › Dealer Fit Packages · 1,777 rows · Open` · `Acts › New quote · N`. The group is the kind, the row is name · fact · verb, and the fact is counted off the store.

**4 · 390.** Four shapes: a full-screen list behind `☰`, seven rows and `Account` at the foot (`hand2/porsche-model-start-390-menu.png`) or four (`hand/attio-390-menu.png`); a tab row overflowing into `…` (`hand/github-repo-390.png`); a header that keeps `☰`, the search glyph and the acts and folds the rail away (`hand/mercury-demo-390-slow.png`); Apple's pill, at the foot on a phone and the top on a tablet. The owner's "bottom bar … is disgusting" was an ACTION bar in a quote flow and does not forbid a phone tab bar, but every board says which shape it draws.

**5 · Back, after picker → configurator → cascade → configurator.** The cascade is a route with `?from=` (`docs/DECISIONS.md`), so Back works; the question is what the shell *says*. Craft: `‹ ›` beside a centred pill naming where you are (`live/craft-home-rail.png`). Linear: the issue's identifier in the head with `1 / 84 ↑ ↓` to its neighbours (`live2/linear-app-shot-head.png`), and `‹ Changelog` — back named for its destination (`live/linear-new-ui.png`). The document already does it with `Back to the build`: the shell's back is a link named for where it goes, and `from=` names it.

## 2. The patterns worth taking, named

**The scope chip.** Linear's 2019 command menu prints what it acts on as a chip above the field — `Issue · LIN-1615 Changelog` — with a single-key shortcut on every row (`live2/linear-new-command-menu-2019.png`); GitHub's palette does the same (`live/github-command-palette-docs.png`). This settles §0.3: one Ctrl K, one finder; on the configurator the chip reads the quote's reference and the first group is that screen's own rows, so today's field becomes the finder's first scope rather than a rival for the key.

**Recent before typing.** Notion's search jumps "to a recently visited page" before a character is typed (`live2/notion-sidebar-scrolled.png`); Stripe's `Shortcuts` — "your pinned and most recently visited pages … where you can pin it" (`live2/stripe-dashboard-shortcuts-section.png`). `docs/LATER.md` already names the old repo's `moduleRecent`, the rail's four most recent places in `prefs`.

**The count or the dot, chosen by the person.** Linear's sidebar settings: reorder, `Always show / When badged / Hide in more menu / Never show`, "with a count or dot" (`live/linear-personalized-sidebar.png`); GitHub's `Issues 5k+ · Pull requests 2.7k` (`live/github-repo-nav.png`).

**Quick Actions as the first row of the rail.** Attio puts `Quick Actions ⌘K` above `Home`, with `/` beside it (`live/attio-home-rail.png`). The finder is found by eye, not only by chord.

**Verb rows and nested pages.** Vercel's `Search Projects…` opens a page inside the menu while `Create New Project…` acts (`live/vercel-dashboard-shortcuts.png`); Raycast's Action Panel is a second Ctrl K inside a result — `↵` the primary act, `Esc` "navigates back from a submenu" (`live/raycast-action-panel.png`). Enter opens a quote found; Ctrl K on it offers its acts.

**The `?` sheet.** Linear's searchable Keyboard Shortcuts panel (`live/linear-keyboard-help.png`); Stripe: "press the question mark key (?) for a list of available keyboard shortcuts" (`hand/stripe-docs-390.png`). The shell owes the whole vocabulary in one searchable sheet, under `pointer: fine` only.

## 3. Type and motion, as observed

Rails are one size and one weight with opacity as the whole hierarchy: Linear's `Workspace ▾` and `Favorites ▾` sit in the row size a shade dimmer (`live/linear-app-shot.png`). The scope chip is a tint of the field, never a colour.

Motion: no still proves an easing. Raycast's `Esc` walks back a submenu — nested pages, never a modal over a modal; Vercel and Geist dim the page behind the menu and put `Esc` inside the field (`live2/geist-command-menu-typed.png`). `dense-tables-and-selection.md` holds the RAIL 100 ms budget and Superhuman's minimal-animation line; a rail that slides slides on every press of every day.

## 4. What to avoid, each with its frame

- **A second head.** Every built screen already spends its masthead (`docs/directions/configurator/built/after-1440-00-stage.png`); Porsche's two-tier bar costs ~200 px (`ref/porsche-top.png`). A bar over those mastheads doubles the head; a bar that replaces them rebuilds eight screens.
- **The mega-menu.** Porsche's `Menu` opens a three-column sheet, the page blurred behind (`live2/porsche-models-menu-open.png`).
- **A rail row for everything.** Mercury runs twelve rows plus four bookmarks (`live2/mercury-demo-slow.png`) against Material's seven. Data is one door, not 53.
- **A finder that only jumps.** GitHub's `/` answers "iss" with one row, `Issues · Jump to` (`finder/github-search-typed.png`). Ours must reach the row, not only the register.
- **A promotion where the menu should be.** Mercury's demo answers `☰` at 390 with "Learn more about Mercury" (`hand2/mercury-demo-390-menu.png`).

## 5. Four directions

Each a different composition **and** a different order and grouping of what the shell carries. Every board owes the finder — scope chip, kind groups, verb rows, recent before typing, the `?` sheet, Back named for its destination — and none puts a shell on Entry.

**A — "The pill".** No rail and no bar: one floating pill top-centre over every screen — `Home · Quotes · Customers · Data · History`, the finder as its trailing bubble — costing no layout height, translucent over a photograph and opaque over a register. The crest is its leading cap; the highlighted word is the current screen, with `‹` at its leading edge named for where it goes. **Exclusive to A:** `live2/apple-hig-tab-bars-ipad-2.png` (the tab bar at the top on a tablet, converting to a sidebar on a button, "a dedicated search tab at the trailing end") and `live/craft-home-rail.png` (the current place as a centred pill between `‹ ›` and `+`). **Only A** puts nothing on any edge, so only A leaves the showroom full-bleed and the register's eighteen rows whole at once. Reflow: 390 — the pill drops to the foot as five tabs, HIG's own phone form, with crest · current · finder pinned at the top drawn as the alternative if the owner rejects a bottom bar on sight; 834 — as drawn; 1920 — centred over a 1440 band. Second dealership: the crest and the tint of the highlighted word; with a different crest the pill is unchanged.

**B — "The rail and its counts".** A 72 px icon rail on the left of every screen but Entry, fixed and identical everywhere: the crest at its head, five doors each with a count or a dot, `New quote` as its act, the finder its first row, `?` and the person at its foot. Grouped by KIND OF PLACE: Showroom doors above a hairline, Cockpit registers below; over a photograph it is the same rail on a translucent band. **Exclusive to B:** `live/m3-navigation-rail.png` (3–7 destinations, an act at the head, the same place on every screen) and `live2/stripe-dashboard-shortcuts-section.png` (a section that fills itself with the pages you visit and lets you pin one). **Only B** counts on the door — open drafts are on the rail before any screen opens. Reflow: 390 — the rail folds into `☰` in the masthead's eyebrow slot and opens a full-height list with the same rows and counts, never a mega-menu; 834 — the 72 px rail; 1920 — pinned open with words. Second dealership: the crest and the accent on the current door's glyph; with no crest, the business's initials in type.

**C — "The finder is the shell".** No persistent navigation: one field in the masthead's eyebrow slot on every screen, resting as the mark with `Ctrl K` beside it, and one act. Pressed, it becomes the finder over the screen — recent places first, the five doors as rows, then whatever is typed grouped by kind with a verb on every row and Ctrl K inside a row for its acts. Ordered by USE: the rows opened this hour before the doors. **Exclusive to C:** `live/raycast-windows.png` (the launcher as the whole interface: name · source · kind, Favourites at zero keystrokes) and `live2/superhuman-palette-shot.png` (a single-key verb on every row, and the published case that "users prefer search to menus"). **Only C** weighs nothing on a photograph and nothing on a register — and only C is the one a clueless user cannot find, so its board must draw the five doors as visible rows the instant the field is pressed. Reflow: 390 — the field is the masthead, full width, the finder a full-screen sheet; 834 and 1920 — the same object. Second dealership: the mark is the field's resting face, so the board is drawn twice, with a wordmark and with the name in type.

**D — "The masthead".** A top bar on the Showroom screens in Porsche's order — the mark alone on its line, `Menu` far left, the person far right — with the screen's own working row beneath. On a Cockpit register the same bar collapses to one line: crest left, the five doors as words, the finder right, so no register loses a row it did not already spend. Grouped by REGISTER: Showroom doors on the bar, Cockpit doors behind `Menu` on a Showroom screen and on the bar in the Cockpit. **Exclusive to D:** `ref/porsche-top.png` (the wordmark centred and alone, the working row beneath, `←` at its leading edge) and `live/highfield-nav.png` (a brand in our file's own band: translucent over full-bleed water, wordmark left, four words, `☰`). **Only D** gives the mark a line of its own. Reflow: 390 — `☰` · crest · person, the menu a full-screen list with `Account` at its foot (`hand2/porsche-model-start-390-menu.png`); 834 — the bar with five words; 1920 — the same bar over centred content. Second dealership: the wordmark and the band's tint; a dealer with only a crest gets the 390 bar at every width.

## 6. The imagery question

**The shell needs none of the photography.** The eight heroes in `heroes-ledger.json` belong to Entry and Home; a hero behind a rail is decoration on a working screen and no board draws one. The 329 catalogue copies are the finder's only picture — a small thumbnail beside a boat result, from copies that all clear 320 px, and the name alone for the quarter of addresses with no held copy.

**The mark is the whole question, and the seed does not carry it.** `marks-ledger.json` holds 18 rows for thirteen BOAT brands — Stabicraft's an error, Mercury white-ink only — and nothing for Northside Marine. So every board's crest and wordmark is `docs/CUSTOMISATION.md`'s identity slot, and every board is drawn twice: with a mark, and with the business name set in type. A brand mark may sit beside a boat result in the finder, Stabicraft's row degrading to its name and Mercury's needing a dark ground. The boards are judged on the second drawing first.
