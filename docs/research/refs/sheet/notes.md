# The sheet — the sweep

Driven 2026-09-22 with `tools/research/capture.ts` into `tools/` (41 frames at 1440 × 900), `boats/` (7 at 1440) and `hand/` (7 at 390 × 844). **55 sources · 55 frames · 3 dead** — Yamaha Australia gave an Imperva hCaptcha wall at 1440 and "Not Found" for the F115 page and for the range at 390, none of it fought. The earlier run left nothing on disk, so nothing here is a re-capture. **Ten stock frames were opened first and eight are dead links, 404s, marketing homes or docs pages** — the index names each; only `ref/tables/apple-compare` and `-2` stand. 45 frames were opened; `sources-index.md` marks the ones that were not, and nothing below cites them.

## 0. What the file asks of this screen — measured

- **Highfield Inflatables**: 588 rows, 33 columns in 8 sections, `hierarchy = series ▸ model ▸ variant`. Seven series, Sport 199 rows down to Coaster 7; **67 models of 4–16 rows, median 9**. `grouping.ts` makes the last level the row's own name, so this is **two drawers and a named row, never a tree** — NN/g's two-level limit met by construction. The Variant column is not one grammar: 50 tokens, `PVC W-W` and `HYP LG-W-DG` but also `540 open (PVC) LG-W-DG`, `Open (HYP) R-B-B` and the words `Dune · Mangrove · Ocean · Polar · Sky`. A cell shows what was typed; nothing parses it into a swatch.
- **Highfield × Yamaha**: 2,519 rows, 11 columns, no hierarchy, display column `Label` (`Highfield - RU230KAM (PVC) WH · Yamaha - F4SMHA`), `Boat`, `Motor` and `Rigging Kit Option` as links. 588 boats at **1–12 motors, median 3**; `Recommended` is true on exactly 588 rows, one per boat.
- **Parts**: 2,937 rows, 17 columns, `category ▸ product`, no picture column. **Dealer Fit Packages**: 1,777 rows, 23 columns, **two columns named `Code` and two named `CTD`**. **Haines Signature**: 9 rows, 24 columns.
- **The engine's heights**: row 28, group line 30, add line 24, heading 34, section band 22, fold chip 132, column floor 116 (`helpers.ts`).
- **What a band costs on Highfield, fully open**: 74 group lines and 67 add lines against 588 leaves — 3,828 px of a 20,292 px scroll, 19 %. With 602 px of room at 1280 × 800 (rail 56, head 64, find 44, heading 34 — a guess until the shell lands) a flat sheet holds 21.5 rows; a median model costs one group and one add line per 9 rows, so **an open two-level sheet reads about 17.6**, and 18 needs the series line sticky or the add line folded into its head. The plan's "two lines per four rows" is the smallest model; the median is two per nine.

## 1. What is genuinely best — the six hard questions

**1 · Three doors from one table.** `tools/notion-tables.png` states the record door in a sentence — "Each row opens up into its own page" — and `tools/notion-galleries.png` the gallery's job: "the best way to display databases with visual components". `tools/nocodb-expand.png` shows the record beside the register with the row still lit, and publishes: "The side panel is the default layout on desktop… on mobile they open as a full-screen drawer." Only Airtable makes the record a thing you build and pay for (`tools/airtable-record-detail.png`); ours derives from the eight sections for nothing.

**2 · Hierarchy as bands.** `tools/aggrid-grouping.png` is the live answer: the levels as removable chips (`Country › Year`), a pinned `Group` column carrying `United States (1109)` and `2008 (263)`, a full row per band. `tools/excel-outline.png` is the cheaper control: a depth ladder `1 2 3` at the gutter's head opens the whole sheet to one level in one press, and a group's summary is a row of the same grid (`Apr Total $881`). Airtable and NocoDB publish the ceiling — "a maximum of 3 levels of grouping (1 main group and 2 subgroups)" (`tools/airtable-grouping.png`), "up to three levels" (`tools/nocodb-groupby.png`). Our engine stops at two drawers; the file never asks for more.

**3 · Edit in place, honestly.** `docs/reference/helmlogic-original.md` §4 already holds the best component in the cohort: read state a real `<button>`, a hover ring that does not shift text, Enter and blur commit, Escape cancels, a blur without change writes nothing, an in-cell pip while saving, and the error as a pill under the cell — *"Not a number"* — never a toast. `tools/mui-editing.png` publishes the way in — "Each cell and row has two modes: edit and view", entered by double-click, Enter, Backspace or Delete ("the latter two options both delete any existing content") or any printable key. `keys.ts` resolves exactly that (`edit-start` with a `seed`, `edit-commit` with a `move`) and `coerce.ts` writes the pill's sentence — `"12abc" is not a number`. `tools/sheets-validation.png` publishes two severities: a value off the list "is rejected", or *Show a warning*. `tools/grist-enter-data.png` adds the paste contract to say aloud: rows past the end "will be added", extra columns "will be omitted".

**4 · A pairing row.** Airtable never shows the join: `tools/airtable-linking.png` draws it from both sides as chips — `Project two` on an action item, `Action item one · three · four` on a project. Grist names four shapes and ours is many-to-many (`tools/grist-refs.png`). So the honest pairing row is a sentence whose two nouns link back to their own rows — which `Label` already is — and the pin is the first identity, the boat, the one with 1–12 rows under it.

**5 · A picture at 24, 32 and 44.** Airtable alone says what a row height does to a picture (`tools/airtable-grid-rowheight.png`): "At the short row height, each record shows one line of text and small attachment thumbnails", taller rows show "larger images", and "changing the row height does not affect the height of field headers". Highfield's held copies are 1024–1100 px top-down renders on white (`boats/highfield-sport-560-specs.png` shows the same renders at the maker): at 24 px a hull is a dash, at 32 a shape, at 44 a hull. So the dense row draws a glyph — *held* or *held as a link*, the packer's verdict in `images.json` — and the picture belongs to 44 and to the record.

**6 · 390 px.** `hand/github-issues-390.png`: the register becomes two-line cards, counts stay figures (`Open 18,571 · Closed 235,435`), the act stays. `hand/apple-compare-390.png`: three columns become two, the third dropped. `hand/aggrid-grouping-390.png`: the pinned group column takes the width and `Athlete | Spo…` is cut. Airtable's grid has "some basic functionality on iOS and Android" (`tools/airtable-grid-top.png`). **The record and the gallery survive in a hand; the register does not** — it becomes the name and one chosen fact, and sideways scroll is refused with a sentence rather than offered.

## 2. Patterns worth taking, named

**The depth ladder** — `1 · 2 · 3` at the gutter's head, every drawer to that level in one press (`tools/excel-outline.png`); ours reads `Series · Model · Every row`.

**The count on every band** — `2008 (263)`, so a shut drawer says what it holds (`tools/aggrid-grouping.png`); `GroupNode.leafCount` carries it.

**The record beside the row** — a side panel on a desk with the row lit, a full-screen drawer in a hand (`tools/nocodb-expand.png`).

**One record shape per table** — "Layouts can't be applied only to specific pages or specific views" (`tools/notion-layouts.png`); the sections are its chapters.

**The mixed set, enumerated** — Figma's *Selection colors* lists each fill once (`tools/figma-mixed.png`); a fill across selected rows shows the distinct values with counts before it writes.

**Space toggles, Enter opens** — a yes/no cell flips on Space, a date opens its calendar on Enter (`tools/grist-enter-data.png`).

**One switch above the sheet** — `METRIC | IMPERIAL` (`boats/highfield-sport-560-specs.png`); ours is the price rung.

**Label over value, four across** — the maker's spec sheet (`boats/highfield-sport-560-specs.png`); Stabicraft's two columns of label · value, right-aligned, a tick for yes (`boats/stabicraft-2100-specs.png`).

## 3. Type and motion, as observed

Off the frames at 1440: Handsontable ~13 px in 30 px rows; Apple's compare a bold label over a regular value at one size, ~17 px, with a hairline under each section head (`boats/apple-compare-specs.png`); Highfield a light label under half again its size in a semi-bold value. Money is right-aligned everywhere except Handsontable's `Salary`, which sits left — an avoid. A still proves no motion; the only published motion is Excel's `ALT+SHIFT+=` / `ALT+SHIFT+-` to open and shut a group, and no frame names a duration. The plan's rule stands: nothing moves while a caret is in a cell; a fold is a height change, not a slide.

## 4. What to avoid, each with its frame

- **A compare that shows nothing** — three `Your boat` dropdowns over 400 px of white (`../picker/zodiac-comparator.png`).
- **A grid inside a row** — "rows that expand… another grid is displayed" (`tools/aggrid-master-detail.png`): the two-level limit spent on a table.
- **A pin that reorders** — "when 'Country' is pinned, it jumps to the first position" (`tools/aggrid-column-pinning.png`); `sections.ts` cuts the run around the pin and moves nothing.
- **A pager on five rows** — `Rows per page: 100 · 1–5 of 5` (`tools/mui-editing.png`).
- **Every column its own widget** — pills, bars, stars, a caret in every cell (`tools/handsontable-demo.png`).
- **A modal on the spec table and a bottom bar under it** — the cookie sheet covers the specifications while `SPORT 560 · REQUEST A QUOTE · DEALER LOCATOR` share 44 px at the foot (`hand/highfield-sport-560-390.png`); the owner has already named that bar.

## 5. Four directions

**A — "The outline".** The register rests: one full-width grid ordered as the file, the hierarchy as bands with a depth ladder at the gutter's head and a count on every band; identity pinned; sections fold to chips; 28 px rows. The record is a side panel that keeps the row lit; the gallery a toggle in the head. **Exclusive to A:** `tools/excel-outline.png` (the `1 2 3` ladder, a group's summary as a row of the same grid) and `tools/aggrid-grouping.png` (levels as removable chips, the count on the band, the group column pinned). **Only A** shows all 588 rows in one scroll and pays for it in the open — 17.6 rows at 1280 × 800 on the file's median model. Reflow: 1920 — more columns before the chips; 834 — the pin plus four columns, the rest folded; 390 — the name and one chosen fact, bands kept, record full-screen. Second dealership: density and band ink only; A draws no picture.

**B — "One card per model".** The gallery rests and the unit is the **model, not the row**: 67 Highfield cards under seven series headings — the held render or the mark plate, the model name, its row count, its materials and its lowest price read through `priceReadOf`. A card opens to its 4–16 rows as a small register with the same cells and the same edit; `Every row (588)` is the last door, labelled. **Exclusive to B:** `tools/notion-galleries.png` (the gallery as the door for a visual table, card preview plus properties) and `boats/highfield-sport-560-specs.png` (a render per colourway captioned with the very tokens our Variant column holds — `B-B-B · B-B-DB · B-W-C · DG-G-MB`). **Only B** never shows the 588 whole unless asked, and only B is a picture screen on a table with pictures and a counted-card screen on one without. Reflow: 1920 — five across; 834 — three; 390 — a one-up list of 96 px cards, a model's rows full-screen. Second dealership: pictures and mark are theirs; 32 of 67 Highfield models already hold none, so the mark plate is drawn first; Parts, Dealer Fit and every join have no picture column, so their card is the category, its row count and its price band.

**C — "The record, and the rail".** The record rests: one row at full width as a spec sheet, values under the table's own section headings, label over value four across, every value a button that becomes a field on Enter with the pill beneath on refusal. A narrow rail lists names grouped series ▸ model; `Compare` puts a second and third row beside the first as columns with a chooser at each head. **Exclusive to C:** `boats/apple-compare-specs.png` (the record as a column, three side by side, a section heading with a hairline, a chooser to swap the row) and `tools/grist-card.png` (the record card with `Form · Compact · Blocks` and an *Edit card layout* the table owns). **Only C** answers "what is this row" with no grid on screen, and only C is the dealer's own spec page for a hull the maker has none for. Reflow: 1920 — rail 280, record 900, compare three-up; 834 — rail 240, one record; 390 — the rail is the screen, a name opens the record full-screen, compare drops to two. Second dealership: the record's picture where held, else the mark; a Dealer Fit record reads `Code (Identity)` and `Code (Primary Accessory)` off the sections, so nothing depends on names being unique.

**D — "Two identities".** For the 8,679 pairing rows first: a master list of the first identity on the left — 588 hulls with counts, `RU230KAM (PVC) WH · 3 motors`, the recommended one starred from `Recommended` — and on the right the second identity's rows for the chosen hull, `Motor · Rigging kit · Prop · Engine hole · Origin` at 28 px. On a boat table the master is series ▸ model and the detail its variants; on Parts the master is the category. **Exclusive to D:** `tools/airtable-linking.png` (the pairing drawn from both sides as chips, the join never shown) and `tools/grist-refs.png` (four relationship shapes named, and a *Reference List* column). **Only D** reads a pairing as the dealer says it — this hull, these motors — and makes the recommended row a fact of the list rather than a column. Reflow: 1920 — master 360; 834 — master 280; 390 — the master is the screen, a hull opens its rows full-screen. Second dealership: density and accent only; D draws no picture.

Owed by all four: the in-cell pill, the mixed set before a fill, UNDO on every fill, Space to peek (`../quotes/live/linear-peek.png`, a built screen's primary, cited as engine behaviour), and a sentence where sideways scroll is refused in a hand.

## 6. The imagery question

**Measured on disk.** `images.json`: 453 addresses, 329 held. Highfield: 534 of 588 rows carry an address, **292 rows are held**, 115 distinct copies at 1024–1100 px — **35 models hold several** (UL260: 4 copies for 8 rows, one per colourway shared across PVC and HYP) and **32 hold none**. Haines 8 of 9 rows; Yamaha 203 of 209, 45 distinct at 800 px; Stacer 60 of 91. Parts, Dealer Fit and the 28 joins have **no picture column**. Eight heroes at 1200–2560 px; 18 mark rows over 13 brand ids, Stabicraft an error entry.

**What the sheet can honestly draw.** No hero belongs on a Cockpit register; there is no stage. A 44 px row can carry a held render; a 24 px row carries only the verdict. B's card draws the catalogue copy at 320–400 px, which every held Highfield copy clears. The maker prints a render per colourway captioned with the code, so the code is the maker's word and a picture per variant *exists* — but the pack holds it for 35 models and for none of the four undecoded tokens, so a colourway cell shows the code and, where held for that row, the copy. **A and D need no picture at all, and saying so is the answer**; B must draw its no-picture card first; C uses the record's picture where held and the mark where not.

## 7. The published numbers against the ported engine (written at the build, 2026-09-22)

The critic (`critique-m2.md` §6) found this sweep cited `docs/reference/dense-tables-and-selection.md` zero times, the one document written for this screen, and named three of its rules absent and one a live disagreement. Read against `src/domain/catalogue/table/helpers.ts` at the build, four numbers disagree, and each is decided:

- **150 px column floor (Grafana) against `FIT_MIN_COL_W = 116`.** The engine's floor was measured twice on the real file — a 28 px private floor took Stacer's clipped values from 26 to 119 under one press of FIT, and 116 is where a 12 px face and the cell's own inset still read. Grafana's 150 is a default for panels of few columns; 33 columns at 150 is 4,950 px against 1,280. **The engine's 116 stands.** A value that still cannot fit ellipsises with its whole self as the cell's title.
- **32 px default row (Retool's ladder) against `ROW_H = 28`.** The register had already argued 28 and measured it; a grouped Cockpit screen at 1280 × 800 owes eighteen rows under its band heads and 32 does not hold them. **28 rests**; 36 and 48 are the two steps a dealer may choose (`ROW_HEIGHTS` in `outline.ts`), each named by the picture it carries — 24, 32 and 44 px, the sweep's own reading in §1.5 — and neither is the resting state the promise is made at.
- **Fixed layout above twelve columns (Observable) against FIT.** Not a disagreement once read closely: every column has an explicit width from `widthOf`, the header never reflows on scroll, and the sheet scrolls sideways — which IS Observable's fixed layout. FIT is the one press that shares the window out and stops at the floor. **Adopted as stated; it was already true.**
- **WCAG 2.1.4 character-key shortcuts against `keys.ts`.** The engine's own keys are all chords or named keys; the single letters this screen adds — J, K, X, and `/`, `[`, `]` — are bound on the grid alone, the criterion's third exemption ("active only on focus"), and the legend says so in the panel. **The exemption is the honest claim until Milestone 4's preferences carry a remap.** A dealer typing an X into a cell first presses Enter, and the legend says that too; the trade is recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

And the resting arithmetic in §0 is re-run on the first screen rather than the median model: Roll-Up is eight models of four variants, the worst drawer on the file, and at the engine's heights it shows sixteen rows in the room the built screen gives the grid. The add line is folded into its head and the drawer line is 24 rather than 30 (`OUTLINE_GROUP_H`), which is the sweep's own second fix taken and one more; the density ruler's reading is in `docs/SCREENS.md`.
