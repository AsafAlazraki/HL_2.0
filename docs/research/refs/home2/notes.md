**Figures.**
- Revolut sets one figure at hero size ("AU$12,024"), with "Personal" small above it and one white pill below.
- Fitness tints each figure in its kind's ink: 13,818 in violet, 6.58 MI in blue. That is the "more colour" the owner asked for, and it is already the idea behind the kit's `Stat`.

**Motion.**
- Arc's band has a wave-cut edge, film grain on electric blue, and a pastel glow (`live/arc-home.png`).
- Linear lights its black from one edge (`live/linear-home-scrolled.png`).
- Toast draws one hand-drawn circle around one figure (`live/toast-now.png`).

**For a dealership:**
- **Suits:** grain and a cut edge on the existing water band; light from one side; one authored moment (Lottie or Rive) when Northside's mark lands.

## 4. What to avoid, each with its frame

- **The invented KPI.** `gallery/dribbble-dashboard.png`: Outcrowd's "$41,5" and "75%", Nixtio's stats laid over a photograph of a player, Phenomenon's gauges. This is what galleries mean by a dashboard.
- **The trend with no past.** `live/toast-now.png` compares "$16,203.22 ↑5%" with "Wed Aug 9". On day one there is no last Wednesday. Its "Labour cost $3,888.77 24%" is cost on the customer's side of the desk.
- **Words over the photograph.**
  - `live/monzo-home.png`: the headline on the photograph's darkened half.
  - `live/revolut-home.png`: the line across the woman's hair, near 4.5:1.
  - `live/robinhood-home.png`: the headline across a face.
- **The foot bar.**
  - My Porsche (`live/porsche-newsroom-app.png`), Tesla (`gallery/appstore-tesla.png`) and Things' floating `+` (`../quotes/live3/things-scrolled.png`) all keep their acts at a phone's foot.
  - Take their hero objects and leave their feet.
- **Customise-your-overview.** Stripe's Home is widgets added and removed (docs.stripe.com/dashboard/basics). A salesperson should not have to compose a dashboard.
- **Generic illustration.**
  - Family's mascots (`live/family-home.png`) and Getillustrations (`gallery/dribbble-empty-state.png`) belong to nobody.
  - Family's state rail, Submitted · Pending · Completed (`live/family-home-scrolled.png`), is worth keeping.

## 5. Day one, the most beautiful state

**What day one honestly holds:** a name, a business, seventeen makers' marks, eight stage photographs and one act. Nothing has moved yet, and nobody is owed a call.

**One object, not three explanations.**
- Things and Home open on their mark, large and centred (`live/things-home.png`, `live/apple-home-app.png`). Recent's "Inbox 0" makes the zero a blue-and-white drawing (`gallery/recent-design.png`).
- Day one is:
  - Northside's mark on the live water at that size
  - the greeting and the date beside it
  - **New quote**
  - capsules reading "None open · Nothing given yet · No customers filed"

**The makers take the room the empty jobs would.**
- HelmLogic-02's marks at card width and Arc's pinned tiles: the makers large, boats and motors alike, each a door into the picker.
- This is true on day one, and stakeholders already know it.

**Never a lesson in a column.**
- `now/01-day-one-1440x900.png` teaches three steps in the window's narrowest quarter. No reference teaches on its first screen.
- "All Done" and "All Off" each say the state in a word or two. The one sentence about where the work is kept stays (`../home/live2/empty-excalidraw.png`).
