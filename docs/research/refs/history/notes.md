# History — the sweep

Driven 2026-09-22 with `tools/research/capture.ts` into `live/` (1440 × 900) and `hand/` (390 × 844). **41 frames on disk · 43 opened · 11 dead or covered** (three GitHub 404s on a wrong PR number, Day One's bot wall twice, Dropbox 404, Tower and 1Password under a consent card, Up painting nothing at either width, Notion's and Wise's 404s in the quotes stock); each is named in `sources-index.md` and none is cited. Paths are relative to this folder; `../quotes/…` is the register's sweep, `ref/…` is `C:\Users\Asaf\dev\hl-refs\ref\`.

**The job, read off the domain first.** `history.ts` gives three standings (draft · given · replaced, the third derived from `supersedesId`, never stored), lineages by union-find so a fork reads as "version 2 and version 3 of the same conversation", `versionMark` as `[2, 3]`, day heads `Today` / `Yesterday` / `Fri 21 Aug 2026`, five spans, a customer facet with `NO_CUSTOMER` as its own honest pile, and a tally that is only ever a length. `quote.ts` puts a typed `QuoteEvent` log on every document — kind, instant, `by`, `changed[]` by value, and `said`, the sentence the toast said, verbatim. So the screen never writes a sentence about an event; it prints `Yamaha F90LB put on the quote · $14,330`, `Priced at Trade`, `A new version of 20260817-01`, `20260824-01 is issued`, `For Rob Kelleher`. `again.ts` gives `quoteAgain` (a NEW quote at today's prices, the customer re-frozen from the register) and `whyNotAgain`, one sentence or empty. History is TIME; the register is STATE.

## 0. Corrections, by looking

1. **GitHub prints two calendars on one page.** `live/github-pr-20463-commits.png`: the head reads `Commits on Dec 14, 2020`; every row under it reads `committed on Dec 15, 2020`. The exact fault `src/domain/quote/day.ts` was written for. This screen's ruler should assert the head and its rows agree.
2. **Linear's activity is behind a login and its audit log behind a plan.** Public: the changelog's rule — "group similar consecutive events and collapse older activity between comment threads" (`live/linear-collapsed-history.png`) — and `live/linear-audit-log.png`, Enterprise only, 90 days, rows printing `inviteAccepted`, `teamPrivacyChanged`, `userLeftTeam`. Ours have `said`; a code name never reaches a screen.
3. **Jira names one thing three ways by audience** — *Issue History* in the UI, *Changelog* in the API, *Changegroup and Changeitem* in the database (`live/jira-history.png`). The dealer sees the first.
4. **No frame shows an "order again" refusal.** Amazon, Uber and Steam need a login; Netlify and 1Password publish the act (§1 Q4), not the refusal. `whyNotAgain` stands on `docs/reference/explaining-a-refusal.md` alone.

## 1. What is genuinely best — the five hard questions

**1 · Forty events on three quotes: feed, ledger or calendar?** A spine with the day as its head and the events FOLDED per quote. `live/github-commits.png` is the shape: `Commits on Sep 18, 2026` as a node on a vertical spine, rows beneath, the head carrying the absolute date and each row the relative one (`authored 4 days ago`), a `Verified` word and a mono id far right. What keeps forty readable is the fold: `live/github-pr-20463-scrolled.png` prints `eps1lon added 21 commits 6 years ago` as ONE line on the spine with the 21 beneath, and Linear publishes the same rule. Our day of forty becomes three lines, one per quote touched, each counting its own kinds in words — `7 picks · 1 put back · priced at Trade · issued` — opening in place to the sentences the events said. The ledger is the owner's "still feels like a database": `live/fork-home.png` runs graph · message · author · sha · `1 Apr 2021 03:13` at one weight, and nothing reads first. The calendar loses in its own house: Google's help names Schedule, the list by day, as the view for a screen reader (`live/gcal-views.png`), and GitHub uses the month grid only as the way BACK to a day (`live/github-commits-daterange.png`: `Sep ▾ 2026 ▾`, `Clear · Today`).

**2 · A chain without a diagram.** Fork and GitKraken draw the chain as coloured rails with avatars on the nodes (`live/fork-home.png`, `live/gitkraken-client.png`) — legible to nobody who does not already read git. GitHub reads the same chain as a SENTENCE — `bvaughn merged 104 commits into react:master from eps1lon:… on Dec 23, 2020` — with counts on the tabs, `Commits 104 · Files changed 50` (`live/github-pr-20463.png`). Ours is already sentences: the `versioned` event says `A new version of 20260817-01`, `versionMark` says `[2, 3]`, `versionsOf` lists the lineage oldest first with each version's day. A row reads `v2 of 3 · replaces 20260817-01`; a quote's page lists its versions as dated lines, the current `Given`, the earlier `Replaced`, a fork as two lines under one root.

**3 · Verb, object, figure, instant — which earns the weight.** The sentence, because the contract fused verb, object and figure into it. `live/github-commits.png` gives the title full weight and everything else one grey line; `live/sublime-merge.png` runs message over author with the time set right, three tones, nothing else. So `said` at full weight, `by` and the time in the grey line, the reference in mono at the right where GitHub keeps the sha. The instant earns weight in one place, the day head; the figure in one, the quote line's frozen total, right-aligned, tabular, never animated (quotes sweep §3).

**4 · "Quote that again": where, and how it refuses.** On the past thing itself. Netlify's `Publish deploy` sits "on the detail page of any successful deploy… This doesn't trigger a new deploy but instead publishes a previous atomic deploy" (`live/netlify-deploys.png`); Figma's `Restore a previous version` and 1Password's `Restore Item` sit on the version. Ours differs in one word that must be printed: it is NOT a restore. `quoteAgain` mints at TODAY's prices and re-freezes the customer (`again.ts`), so the act is `Quote this again, at today's prices`, on the quote's line or page, never in a day head. Its refusal stands where the button would: `Highfield SP560 is not on the sheet any more, so there is nothing to price. This quote still opens and still prints — it kept its own copy of every number on it.` Stripe puts the reason on the timeline — "see the return reason listed in the Timeline section" (`live/stripe-payouts-timeline.png`) — and so does ours.

**5 · Day one, when nothing has happened.** The old repo's render suite pins the shape (`HL_Playground/src/features/history/render.test.ts`, owed by `docs/LATER.md`): name the place, count what you already have, give one act that works — and draw no filter bar and no drafts shelf when there is nothing to filter. Quotes sweep §5 holds Primer's first-run-versus-error rule and Telwater's *WHAT HAPPENED? / WHAT DO I DO?*. A diary adds one thing: the empty state is a day head — `Today`, then `Nothing has happened yet.`, then `New quote`. Things prints a finished day as a tally, not a blank: `All Done · 12 to-dos completed` (`../quotes/live3/things-scrolled.png`, the watch).

## 2. The patterns worth taking, named

**The day is the head; the age is the row.** `live/github-commits.png`. The absolute date once, on the spine; the row says `4 days ago`; `dayTitle` and `localDay` supply both from one calendar.

**Same-kind events fold into one counted line.** `live/github-pr-20463-scrolled.png`, `live/linear-collapsed-history.png`. Seven `line-added` events are `7 picks`, opened in place; `changed[]` by value is what the open line prints.

**The chain is a sentence with counts.** `live/github-pr-20463.png`. Actor, verb, count, from, into, date; `versionMark` and the `versioned` event's `said` write it.

**The state head sticks.** `hand/github-pr-390.png`: `Merged · title · #20463 · bvaughn merged 104 commits…` stays at the top of a phone while the body scrolls; a quote's diary keeps its standing and reference the same way.

**The way back to a day is a control, not a view.** `live/github-commits-daterange.png`; Netlify's `Last hour · Last day · Last 7 days · Custom` (`live/netlify-deploys.png`) are our `SPAN_TITLE`. A chosen day is `?day=2026-09-22`.

**Changed since you last looked.** `../quotes/live2/google-docs-versions.png`: "a blue dot on the Last edit icon… means someone updated the file since you last viewed it." Per viewer, so `prefs`.

## 3. Type and motion, as observed

Two greys at one size do the work wherever it reads well: GitHub's title black and meta grey (`live/github-commits.png`), Sublime Merge's message / author / time (`live/sublime-merge.png`), Things' grey project line under each item (`live/things-views.png`). Mono is for identifiers — the sha there, the reference `20260922-01` here, which `day.ts` already sets in the mono label's tabular figures. Linear's changelog sets the date in the gutter at body size with a dot on the spine (`ref/tables/linear-changelog.png`). Relative time decays — GitHub's `6 years ago` (`live/github-pr-20463-scrolled.png`) is what a diary must not print; `dayTitle` stops at `Yesterday`.

Motion: 41 frames prove none. What exists is disclosure — a fold opening in place, a popover for the month grid — and both are asked for. A diary ticks (`4 days ago` becomes `5`), so the age re-reads on navigation and never under the eye; nothing moves while a caret is in the find field (`docs/PLAN.md`, UI kit). Reduced motion keeps the fold and drops the slide.

## 4. What to avoid, each with its frame

- **The chain as coloured rails** — `live/fork-home.png`, `live/gitkraken-client.png`.
- **Two calendars on one screen** — `live/github-pr-20463-commits.png`: head `Dec 14`, rows `Dec 15`.
- **Events as code names** — `live/linear-audit-log.png`: `teamPrivacyChanged`, `inviteAccepted`.
- **One weight, five columns** — `live/fork-home.png`; the eye lands nowhere.
- **A history with a window** — Linear's 90 days on Enterprise, Figma's 30 days on Starter (`../quotes/live2/figma-version-history.png`). An issued quote's events are "part of what was handed over" (`quote.ts`); nothing is trimmed.
- **Relative time past a week** — `6 years ago` (`live/github-pr-20463-scrolled.png`).
- **Hidden without a count** — the opposite of `live/sentry-issue-details.png`'s `4 comments hidden`; a folded line says how many events it holds.

## 5. Four directions

Each a different composition **and** a different order and grouping. All print only `said`, `by`, `at`, `versionMark`, `standingOf` and frozen totals; none invents a sentence about an event. Density is stated against the register's measured room, 657 px at 1280 × 800 less 72 px of heads (`src/screens/quotes/quotes.css`, `docs/STATUS.md`).

**A — "The spine".** One vertical spine down a 720 px measure. Day heads are nodes carrying `dayTitle` and the day's counts; under each, ONE folded line per quote touched that day — reference in mono, boat, `7 picks · priced at Trade · issued`, standing word — opening in place to the sentences the events said, each with its time and `by`; the chain inside the fold as `v2 of 3 · replaces 20260817-01`; `Quote this again, at today's prices` or its refusal at the fold's foot. Grouped by day, then quote, then event. **Exclusive to A:** `live/github-pr-20463-scrolled.png` (a conversation spine where 21 consecutive events fold to one counted line, opened beneath) and `live/linear-collapsed-history.png`. **Only A shows what was DONE, event by event, on the day it was done.** Reflow: 390 — spine at the gutter, the meta wrapping under the title as `hand/github-commits-390.png` does; 834 — as drawn; 1920 — the 720 measure centred. Density: 20 folded lines at 28 px under three day heads. Second dealership: the accent on the nodes.

**B — "The day book".** Two columns: a date gutter on the left with the day written once and its tally under it — `3 quotes · 1 given · $66,584 given`, a sum of frozen lines, never a forecast — against a continuous right column of that day's QUOTES as cards led by the boat's held picture, a plate with the mark where there is none, reference and total on the foot. Grouped by day, then by the quote's last event. **Exclusive to B:** `ref/tables/linear-changelog.png` (the date alone in the left gutter with a dot on the spine, the entry in a fixed measure to its right) and `live/apple-photos-by-date.png` (the day as a collection led by its own picture, the count on the head). **Only B answers "what did today look like" without reading a sentence.** Reflow: 390 — the gutter becomes a sticky day head above the cards, Apple's own form; 834 — gutter 200, cards 560; 1920 — gutter 280, cards 800. Density: cards do not hold 18 rows and B says so — `?day=` opens the register's rows for a day. **C — "One quote's diary".** A list of quotes at 320 px on the left, newest touched first, and the chosen quote's diary on the right: its versions as dated entries (`v1 · given Mon 17 Aug · Replaced`, `v2 · given Mon 24 Aug · Given`, `v3 · Draft`), the chosen version's events oldest first as one dated list, then the act on a past thing with `whyNotAgain` in its place when refused. **D — "The month".** A month as a grid of seven columns, each day cell carrying its counts in type — `2 quoted · 1 given` — and a filled mark only where something was given; beneath, the chosen day's quotes as register rows. Grouped by month, then day, then quote; a pressed day is `?day=`. Drawn so the owner can reject it knowingly: it wins getting back to a day and loses the reading of what happened. **Exclusive to D:** `live/github-commits-daterange.png` (the month grid with `Clear · Today` as the way back over a history) and `../quotes/live3/things-scrolled.png` (a day's tally as one figure, `All Done · 12 to-dos completed`). **Only D makes the day a thing you press.** Reflow: 390 — seven 44 px cells fit in 308, rows beneath; 834 — grid 600 centred; 1920 — grid left, rows right. Density: a full grid leaves 12 rows at 28 px, so it collapses to one week strip while rows are read, giving 20. Day one is thirty cells of nothing with a sentence under them. Second dealership: the accent only.

## 6. The imagery question

**A diary needs none of it, and A, C and D draw none.** Measured on disk 2026-09-22: `heroes-ledger.json` holds eight photographs (Highfield ADV7, PA600, SP560, SP600; Stacer 309 Skimma, 359 Territory Striker, 481 SeaMaster, 519 Sea Ranger SDF) — stage pictures, never rows; `public/seed-images` holds 329 catalogue copies capped at 1100; `marks-ledger.json` holds 18 rows over twelve of thirteen brands, Stabicraft with none and Mercury white-ink only.

**B alone wants a picture, and only the exact boat's.** A card may carry the held catalogue copy of the quote's own subject — every held copy clears 320 px, so a 96–240 px lead is served without enlarging (quotes sweep §7) — and about a quarter of addresses are unheld, so B's mark-plate card is its first drawing, not its fallback. A day led by its "best picture" is refused: nothing in the seed says which of a day's quotes deserves the lead, and choosing one would be an invented figure. A second dealership replaces every picture and mark; B's cards change and not a pixel of A, C or D does.
