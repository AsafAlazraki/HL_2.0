# Customers — the sweep

Frames captured 2026-09-22 by a run that hit its account limit before writing a word; read and written the same day by this one. **214 frames · 197 distinct · 45 opened** (the budget), counted from the files; duplicates, walls and recovered URLs are in `sources-index.md`. Paths are relative to `docs/research/refs/customers/`; stock is `C:\Users\Asaf\dev\hl-refs\ref\`. **Only a frame that was opened is cited.**

**The job.** Find a customer; open them; file one by hand; edit their details; see every quote addressed to them with its standing — draft, given, replaced. Cockpit: **18 rows at 1280 × 800**. The register does not exist until somebody makes it.

## 0. Corrections, by looking and by reading the contract

1. **Marine dealer systems publish no customer screen.** Boatyard's only register is Boats, the same placeholder row seven times (`deep/boatyard-sales-900.png`); DockMaster and Lightspeed are checkmark lists over stock photographs (`marine/dockmaster-dealership-scrolled.png`, `marine/lightspeed-evo-marine-scrolled.png`). Nothing marine can be primary.
2. **Twenty's public demo is a login wall** (four frames); Google Contacts, GitHub's new-repository form and Xero are logins; the stock `ref/tables/github-new-repo.png` is GitHub's sign-in page. **No frame shows a register being born.**
3. **Thirty-eight `deep/` frames have no ledger row** — `capture.ts` writes `sources.json` after the last source and the run died first. URLs recovered from its list files are marked in the index.
4. `crm/pipedrive-how-duplicates.png` carries only the heading; its rule is quoted below from the page text.
5. **The contract settles more than a sweep can.** `src/domain/people/customers.ts`: one table with a well-known id, created with Phone, Email, Address (printed on a quote) and Notes ("for the yard — never printed"); the name is whatever `displayFieldOf` names; **rows are never re-sorted**; `matchCustomers` is starts-with then contains, searches the note, caps at 8, and **an empty query returns the head of the list**. `src/state/customerLink.test.ts`: a quote prints from frozen values; the row id answers **one** question, "what else have we quoted them?"; deleting the customer leaves every document whole; filing a typed name **refuses when there is no register**. `src/domain/quote/diary/history.ts`: three standings, *replaced* derived from `supersedesId`; no won, no lost, no sale. The original app (`docs/reference/helmlogic-original.md` §2.7) carried `source`, `lifecycleStage` and a secondary buyer with **no authoring UI anywhere**, and its "Linked quotes" rendered zero for every real proposal — its two users filled in nothing beyond a name and how to reach them.

## 1. What is genuinely best — the five hard questions

**1 · Five lines, drawn without becoming a dashboard.** `deep/apple-contacts-update-scrolled.png` is the best frame: a letter-sectioned list beside a card; in edit state every empty field is a grey placeholder with a label chooser, filled ones carry a red minus, one *Done*. `deep/monica-card-1300.png` is the record at rest: initials, name, one meta line ("Sister · Lyon · Last spoke today"), two labelled facts side by side, RECENTLY as two entries, one foot — "the details that matter to you, not the fields a sales CRM thinks should matter." The failure is in the same sweep: Attio offers "up to six attributes as highlight widgets" and its own example shows three tiles reading *No Connection / No interaction / No interaction* (`deep/attio-highlights.png`); its full record prints "No values" and "No contact" as values in fourteen rows under seven tiles (`deep/attio-record-tabs.png`). GOV.UK's summary list, the document screen's primary, adds the rule the card needs: a missing value gets **Add** beside *Change* — an act, never a placeholder (`deep/govuk-summary-list.png`); `contacts/govuk-names.png` sanctions *Full name* as one field, `contacts/govuk-addresses.png` a textarea for an address, which is what one Address column is.

**2 · Three customers, and three hundred.** No product photographs its register at three rows; the smallest real one in 214 frames is **six** — `crm/linear-manage-members.png`: one search, no column head, no pager, one primary. At scale, `deep/pipedrive-contacts-list-whole.png` prints **"63 people"** beside the list, sorts on *Name ↑*, sets email as a labelled chip and leaves a blank cell blank at about 19 px a row; `deep/hubspot-record-whole.png` prints "165,469 records", a pager and `--` in an empty cell. The arithmetic is the quotes sweep's, from `docs/reference/dense-tables-and-selection.md`: 604 px of room at 1280 × 800 after rail, head, find row and column head; **18 rows at 32 px = 576 fits; a two-line row at 44 px holds 13 and fails the gate**, so the contact line sits beside the name, never under it. Three rows sit at the pitch of three hundred, the count stated, the search present from the first row because `matchCustomers` answers an empty field with "who have I sold to before".

**3 · Every quote for this person, beside their details.** `crm/linear-user-views.png` is the honest answer and the trap: a person's page is their issues grouped by state under their name — our three standings exactly — but it is the quotes register redrawn under a heading, which `docs/SCREENS.md` forbids. `deep/attio-record-preview.png` shows the better grammar: a timeline grouped *This week*, every entry a sentence with its count — "changed 18 attributes · 3 days ago"; `deep/attio-timeline-2026-500.png` puts counts on the tabs with *Upcoming* first. Stripe writes the rule the record must say out loud: details may change "**until an invoice is finalized**" (`deep/stripe-customer-page-3.png`) — `customerLink`'s promise in a billing company's words. A quote row carries the document's own facts off `history.ts` — boat, total, standing as a word, day.

**4 · The first run, and what makes the table.** Atlassian's rule (`empty/atlassian-empty-state-writing.png`): "include the reason for the empty state and where they can go next", one to two sentences; NN/g confirms (`deep/nngroup-empty-states.png`). Shopify says who fills a customer list: "every time a new customer places an order … their name and other details are added"; by hand is the *alternative* (`crm/shopify-customers.png`, `deep/shopify-manage-customers-2.png`). Notion's "select a database type" (`empty/notion-create-database.png`) is vocabulary a dealer must never meet. The contract names ours: the register is "never made by anything except the button that names it", one undo step. So `/customers` with no table says what the book will hold, that nothing is filed because no quote has been addressed yet, and offers one act that makes it; the configurator's refusal until then is rule and fix in one clause (`docs/reference/explaining-a-refusal.md`).

**5 · Two Sarah Jones at the desk, with no modal.** `deep/linear-similar-issues-now-scrolled.png`: possible duplicates are listed **under the create form** while *Create issue* stays live, and the changelog names the verb — "turn your issue draft into a comment on the canonical issue instead" (`deep/linear-similar-issues-changelog-scrolled.png`). Pipedrive publishes the rule (page text): "the same **name** … **and one of the following**: the same phone number, the same email address, or … the same organization" — it flags, never merges. `exactCustomer` matches on the name alone; the second fact separates two people. Apple's verbs — *Keep Old / Keep New / Keep Both* (`contacts/apple-contacts-resolve-duplicates.png`) — with **Keep Both the default**: `customerLink.test.ts` › "two people with the same name are two people". Google: "you can separate a contact that you merged" (`contacts/google-contacts-merge.png`) — merge with undo, not confirm.

## 2. The patterns worth taking, named

**The possible duplicate under the form.** `deep/linear-similar-issues-now-scrolled.png`. As a name is typed, matching rows appear beneath the fields with their contact line, each an act, and filing stays live.

**The count beside the list; a pager only when the room runs out.** `deep/pipedrive-contacts-list-whole.png`, `deep/hubspot-record-whole.png`.

**A list with no column head.** `crm/linear-manage-members.png`.

**An absent value is an act, not a tile.** `deep/govuk-summary-list.png` (*Add* beside a missing contact), `deep/apple-contacts-update-scrolled.png` (placeholders only while editing). "No values" as a value (`deep/attio-record-tabs.png`) is the failure.

**The change counted, and grouped by when.** `deep/attio-record-preview.png`: *This week* as the group, "changed 18 attributes" as the entry.

**The frozen-detail sentence.** `deep/stripe-customer-page-3.png`. Under the fields: *A change here is used on the next quote. Quotes already given keep what they were given.* And `STANDING_SAY` under a filter that empties.

**Keep both, and unmerge.** `contacts/apple-contacts-resolve-duplicates.png`, `contacts/google-contacts-merge.png`.

**One field over two books.** `deep/boatyard-sales-900.png`'s placeholder, *Search Boats or Clients*; ours finds "the one with the blue Hilux" by the note, as `matchCustomers` already does.

**The record opened beside the row.** `deep/attio-record-preview.png`, "without leaving your current view" (Airtable's sidesheet and Linear's peek: `dense-tables-and-selection.md`).

**Initials as the honest avatar.** `crm/linear-profile.png`: "the first and last initials".

**Edit in the cell, error as a pill.** `helmlogic-original.md` §4: read state a real button, Enter and blur commit, Escape cancels, the error under the cell, never a toast.

## 3. Type and motion, as observed

Two greys again: Pipedrive's rows and Linear's members set the name full and the email grey; Apple's card sets the label small and blue, the value black, the placeholder grey; Monica labels in small-caps mono over body-weight values; GOV.UK bold key, regular value, underlined act. The only money on a record is each quote's total, right-aligned in the body face; in 45 frames it never animates, and Pipedrive's $7,900 in a green pill is the one place a figure earns colour. Motion: none proven; every timeline is a still. Nothing moves while a caret is in a field (`docs/PLAN.md`); the record sliding beside the row is the one motion worth having, under RAIL's 100 ms.

## 4. What to avoid, each with its frame

- **A dashboard for a person** — seven tiles and fourteen attribute rows (`deep/attio-record-tabs.png`); three tiles reading "No interaction" (`deep/attio-highlights.png`).
- **A won/lost bar** (`deep/pipedrive-person-detail-whole.png`) — `history.ts` has no sale; drawing one invents it.
- **A placeholder printed as a value** — "No values", "No contact" (`deep/attio-record-tabs.png`); "You have no upcoming activities" in a pill (`deep/pipedrive-person-detail-whole.png`).
- **A wall of chips per row** (`deep/attio-understanding-records-scrolled.png`); **the same fake row seven times** (`deep/boatyard-sales-900.png`).
- **A similarity score** — "30% and up", "122 total duplicate issues" (`deep/hubspot-manage-duplicates-2400.png`): a figure nobody measured from the file.
- **A merge that "intelligently combines your data"** in a modal (`deep/attio-merge-compare.png`), even drawn as A + B = C.
- **The data-model vocabulary at the door** — "select a database type" (`empty/notion-create-database.png`), Objects · Fields · Records (`deep/twenty-docs-people-object.png`).
- **Email required to exist** — HubSpot's *Email \** (`deep/hubspot-record-whole.png`); a walk-in with a phone number is a customer.

## 5. Four directions

Each a different composition **and** a different order and grouping. None draws a picture, so a second dealership changes the accent (selected row, initials disc, group head), the row pitch and the face, and nothing else. The mark lives in the shell, never on a person.

**A — "The card index."** A narrow alphabetised list on the left, letters as section heads, name only, one search over it; the CARD on the right at a form's width: initials and name, then the table's columns as label · value rows **in the register's own column order**, a hairline, then the quotes as dated rows. Grouped by letter in the list, facts-then-quotes on the card; edit in place with one *Done*. **Exclusive to A:** `deep/apple-contacts-update-scrolled.png` (the sectioned list beside a card whose empty fields are placeholders with a label chooser) and `deep/apple-hig-lists-live.png` (the grouped table view with a header **and a footer** — where a section's sentence lives: the note's *never printed on a quote*). **Only A shows list and record at once**, so the next customer is one key away and two Sarah Jones are adjacent rows under J before anything is typed. Reflow: 1920 — list 320, card 640, centred; 834 — list 280 beside the card; 390 — the list is the screen, a row opens the card full-screen.

**B — "The sheet itself."** Not a new screen: the register drawn as what `customers.ts` says it is — a table with the dealer's own columns, the name pinned left, editing in the cell, one computed column reading *3 quotes · last 12 Sep*, and the record not a page but the row expanded in place. Grouped by nothing; ordered as the table is. **Exclusive to B:** `deep/pipedrive-contacts-list-whole.png` (the count beside the list, sort on the name column, a blank cell left blank) and `deep/twenty-home-900.png` (a *Calculate ▾* footer cell — the aggregate at a column's foot, ours the count). Built on `dense-tables-and-selection.md` and the original's inline-edit cell. **Only B lets a dealer who adds "ABN" see it as a column the same minute**, and only B should be rejected knowingly: it wins "all tables should be a module" and loses "the tables — still too complicated". If the M2 sheet sweep claims this shape, B is the sheet pointed at `__customers`. Reflow: 1920 — more of the dealer's columns; 834 — name pinned, the rest scrolls sideways (the only direction that does); 390 — not a table: name and first contact line per row, the record full-screen.

**C — "The letter."** Record-first: the screen opens on the last customer touched (or the empty state) as one centred page — the name as heading, **the address printed as the document will print it**, phone and email on one line, the note in a tinted band saying *for the yard — never printed*, then every quote as a row led by a miniature of its own A4 cover from the document screen's render, grouped **by lineage** (`history.ts` versions, latest first). Finding is a field above the page listing eight names as you type; *All customers* is a door, not the resting screen. **Exclusive to C:** `deep/monica-card-1300.png` (one card: identity line, two labelled facts side by side, a short RECENTLY, one foot) and `deep/stripe-customer-page-3.png` (the record saying that a change here reaches no given quote). **Only C shows the dealer the typo before the customer does.** Reflow: 1920 — a 720 px measure, miniatures three-up; 834 — 640, two-up; 390 — one column, miniature dropped, row kept (§6 for how the miniature degrades).

**D — "Who is at the desk."** The register as **columns, not rows**: *On a draft (n) · Quoted, waiting (n) · Replaced (n) · Not yet quoted (n)* — grouped by the standing of each person's **latest** quote off `history.ts`, ordered by what the dealer does next. A card is initials, name, latest boat and total; the record opens beside the columns, its quotes Draft first. **Exclusive to D:** `crm/folk-home.png` (people in stage columns with the person panel opening beside them) and `deep/attio-timeline-2026-500.png` (tabs carrying counts, *Upcoming* first). **Only D answers "who am I working for today" from the register**, and only D pre-empts the M5 pipeline's shape — drawn so the owner can say whether customers and pipeline are one screen. Reflow: 1920 — four columns; 834 — four stacked bands; 390 — four tabs with counts.

Owed by all four, from §2: the possible duplicate under the filing form, *Keep both* as the default, the count, the frozen-detail sentence, and §1.4's first-run screen.

## 6. The imagery question

**A customer needs none of it, and that is the answer.** Measured on disk: `heroes-ledger.json` holds eight on-water photographs (four Highfield, four Stacer, up to 2560 px); `public/seed-images` 329 catalogue copies at long edge ≤ 1100; `marks-ledger.json` seventeen marks for twelve of thirteen brands. None depicts a person, and none may be invented — the avatar is initials (§2); a brand mark never leads a customer row. A picture may honestly appear only on the **quote rows** of a record: the held catalogue copy of that quote's boat at 64–96 px, which every held copy clears (quotes sweep §7), with a quarter of addresses unheld — so the no-picture row is designed first. C's miniature is the document's own cover and degrades as the document does; A, B and D draw nothing, so a second dealership replacing every photograph changes C's miniatures and not a pixel elsewhere.
