# Settings (`/manage`): the sweep

Driven 2026-10-29. **34 frames captured · 34 Read calls** (30 new, 4 stock). Only an opened frame is cited, and paths are relative to `docs/research/refs/manage/`. `sources-index.md` lists every frame, every miss and every refusal.

**The job.** This is the one place Northside's manager makes the app and its paper Northside's own, without a developer. It holds five things:

1. **The details** the letterhead prints.
2. **The few people** who sell.
3. **How the app looks**, bounded and measured.
4. **One door** to every other setting.
5. **Home's arrangement.**

The best proof a manager can have is the change landing on the real thing before it is kept.

## 1. What is genuinely best: the five hard questions

**1 · What makes it feel like the business, not a form?**
- **The business's own artefacts at the size they are used.**
  - `live/ghost-design-settings.png` gives two thirds of the window to the live site ("News Wave", its real masthead). A one-third panel carries Brand · Theme tabs, with each asset beside its own thumbnail.
  - `live/canva-brand-kit-panel.png` opens on the mark large on its ground, then *Identity* as three specimen tiles: the logo on its colour, the palette as bars, "Aa". No field shows until one is opened.
- **The failure is equal-weight rows** (`live/linear-preferences.png`). They suit one person's switches, but would make the letterhead read like a cursor preference.
- Northside's own register is loud: geometric caps, a blue duotone over the yard, a yellow band and "Putting people on the water since 1965" (`live/northside-contact.png`).
- Ours: the mark, the water and the paper lead, and a field is what a press reaches.

**2 · A chooser where every choice is measured and some are refused.**
- `live/leonardo-theme.png` prints each swatch's ratio on the swatch (1.45 · 2.05 · 3.03 · **4.54** · 7 · 10.86 : 1).
- `live/radix-custom-palette.png` derives two 12-step scales from three inputs and labels the steps by JOB: *Backgrounds · Interactive components · Borders and separators · Solid colors · Accessible text*. That is CUSTOMISATION.md's derived scale, already grouped.
- `live/contrast-finder.png` refuses #F5A623 on white at ✗ **2.03**. It then offers "40 results (350 colors tested)", nearest first, each with ratio and **distance** (#916214 · 5.30 · 109.64).
- Ours: one sentence at the swatch ("This amber reads 2.0 : 1 on white paper; text needs 4.5"), then one to three pressable passes, each with its ratio.

**3 · How a manager SEES a change land.**
- On the surface itself, with a switch between surfaces. Ghost's `Homepage · Post` tabs over its preview are the pattern.
- `live/apple-ios18-home.png`: one tint redraws every icon over the person's own photograph.
- `live/square-receipt-customise.png` is the paper case: the brand colour as the head band, the logo on it, and the name and address at the foot.
- Ours: the document screen's component draws the most recent real draft beside the fields. With no draft, a sentence says so.

**4 · Three people, not an empty table.**
- As the cards they print. `live/hubspot-signature.png` is `Prepared by` line for line: name, title and company over a hairline, then phone, email, web and address.
- `live/apple-ios17-contact-poster.png` sets a name large, in a colour taken from the pixels behind it.
- Three cards across, with "Add a person" as the fourth slot, never a blank table.

**5 · 390.**
- `hand/radix-custom-390.png` stacks the inputs full width with the derived scale beneath, so the effect stays under its cause.
- `hand/invoice-generator-390.png` is the failure: an ad box covers the form. Nothing floats over a field being typed in, and the shell's pill is that risk.

## 2. Patterns worth taking, named

**The paper is the form.** `live/invoice-generator.png` lays out "+ Add Your Logo", "Who is this from?", Bill To and Payment Terms where they print. Pressing the ABN on the letterhead edits the ABN. Unlike that frame, an empty field prints nothing, and the hint lives only in the editor.

**The surface switch over the preview.** `live/ghost-design-settings.png`. Ours is `Door · Home · Quote · A place`.

**One seed, a scale grouped by job.** `live/radix-custom-palette.png`: one colour in, the steps shown under ground · edge · act · ink.

**The ratio on the swatch.** `live/leonardo-theme.png`. Each ratio is measured against the ground the colour will actually sit on: paper, room or water.

**The nearest that passes.** `live/contrast-finder.png`: the refusal, the count tested, and the alternatives by distance.

**The colour from the photograph.** `live/material-theme-builder.png` (a wallpaper becomes the "Source color") and `live/apple-ios17-contact-poster.png`. Ours is the scrim computed from the hero's pixels, shown as a figure beside it: "the greeting reads 7.1 : 1 here".

**Named slots, sized in words.** `live/shopify-brand-assets.png` (Default logo · Square logo · Cover image) and Ghost's "at least 60x60px". Ours:
- *Mark for paper (dark ink)*
- *Mark for the water (light ink)*
- *Door background*
- *Home's hero*

A white-only file in the paper slot is refused with Mercury's sentence.

**Both inks side by side.** `live/canva-brand-kit-scrolled.png` ends on one mark on four grounds. Northside's mark is always drawn on paper and on water.

**The theme drawn as the product.** `live/rayso-themes.png`: each theme is Raycast's own window, with four dots and an author. Each face in our list is a quote line set in it.

**Visibility and order as one row.** `live/linear-preferences.png`'s "App sidebar · Customize item visibility, ordering". Home's sections each get an eye and a handle. A hidden section stays as a quiet "Hidden · Show" line, so it comes back in one press.

**Arrange on the thing.** `live/apple-ios16-lockscreen.png` and `live/apple-ios18-control.png`: the surface is arranged in place, with no list of settings. That is Home's own "Arrange" press.

**The merchant's ground on the customer's page.** `live/stripe-invoicing-hero.png`: the payer sees the merchant's icon on its dark ground, a white card with To · From · Memo, and the act in the accent.

## 3. Type and motion

- **The business speaks at display size, and the controls are small.**
  - Canva sets the mark and "Identity" before any control.
  - HubSpot titles its tool in a serif.
  - Apple's poster sets the name heavy and condensed, about a third of the screen wide.
- **Small labels over large specimens.**
  - Radix numbers its steps in ~11px grey.
  - Leonardo prints its ratios at ~10px.
  - Ghost's help lines run near 10px, which is too small: nothing goes under `--text-2xs`.
- **Northside's register** is display caps with a marker-hand eyebrow ("SERVICE", `live/northside-contact-scrolled.png`). It tells us the tone. The faces are not ours, and the bounded list ships with the app.
- **Motion.** None is provable from stills; Canva's tiles carry a pause control. What the person needs:
  - The preview redraws instantly on each change, and no figure animates.
  - A kept change gets one toast with Undo.
  - A refusal gets only its sentence.
  - "Full or reduced" is itself a setting, and the operating system's "reduce" wins.

## 4. What to avoid, each with its frame

- **The modal before the tool:** `live/shadcn-create.png` blurs the editor behind "Get Started".
- **The placeholder printed as content:** `document/live/xero-invoice-template.png` circles "Your Company Name", and `live/invoice-generator.png` prints "Who is this from?". An empty terms or deposit field prints nothing.
- **Invented people:** `live/radix-custom-scrolled.png` ("Emily Adams", twice) and `live/hubspot-signature.png` ("111 222 3333"). We draw the real draft and person, or a sentence.
- **A chooser that silently shows something else:** `live/whocanuse.png` was asked for F5A623 and drew its own 663399, scoring AAA. Never substitute; refuse in words.
- **Every setting the same row:** `live/linear-preferences.png`, where nine equal rows end with the theme.
- **Something floating over the form at 390:** `hand/invoice-generator-390.png`.
- **A text page as a reference:** `document/live/stripe-invoice-customize.png`.

## 5. Four directions

Every board owes these, and none of the four is narrow-list-left, detail-right:
- the letterhead on the most recent real draft, drawn by `src/screens/document`'s component, or the sentence;
- both inks of Northside's mark, with provenance;
- one refused colour with its sentence and nearest pass;
- Home's sections with an eye and a handle;
- the door to Rules · Prices · Places · Shelf · Templates.

**A · "The letterhead"**
- **Composition.** One A4 sheet at true scale, centred on the floor, is the stage. Every printed thing is pressable and opens its editor in place: the mark, the name, the ABN, the address, Prepared by, validity, GST, deposit wording and terms. The margins carry notes such as "From the price file; set here to override" and "Frozen on each quote at issue".
- **Order.** Head → Prepared by (people are a switch on that line) → foot. Appearance and Home are two folded bands below.
- **Exclusive references.** `live/invoice-generator.png` and `live/square-receipt-customise.png`.
- **Only A** puts every field where the customer will read it.
- **Reflow.**
  - 1920: the sheet at 210mm with notes in both margins.
  - 834: full width, a note under the pressed field.
  - 390: the sheet scaled to fit. A press opens a bottom sheet with the field and the paper fragment enlarged, and the pill stays clear.
- **What Northside changes.** The mark, the accent (rule and heading ink), the face and the terms.

**B · "The room and the panel"**
- **Composition.** The live app fills two thirds: door, Home, a quote or a place, chosen by a switch on the preview's top edge. The right third is one panel, *Brand · Pictures · Shape · Home · Details*. The Home tab puts handles and eyes on the previewed sections. The Details tab previews the quote.
- **Order.** Appearance first, Home second, details last.
- **Exclusive references.** `live/ghost-design-settings.png` and `live/material-theme-builder.png`.
- **Only B** shows one accent landing on four surfaces in turn without leaving the page.
- **Reflow.**
  - 1920: preview 1,360 and panel 440.
  - 834: preview above at 16:10, panel below.
  - 390: the preview is a 56%-height window, and the panel is a bottom sheet with a segmented bar.
- **What Northside changes.** All of it, seen on the real screens.

**C · "The brand book"**
- **Composition.** One page of full-width plates:
  1. *Identity*: the mark on paper and on water.
  2. *Colour*: one input, the scale by job, a ratio on every swatch, the refusal and its passes.
  3. *Pictures*: the eight heroes as a strip with provenance and a scrim figure each.
  4. *Type*: faces as quote lines.
  5. *Shape*: named radius and shadow steps on a real card.
  6. *Motion*.
  7. *People*.
  8. *Details*, ending on the letterhead.
- **Order.** By kind of asset.
- **Exclusive references.** `live/canva-brand-kit-panel.png` and `live/leonardo-theme.png`.
- **Only C** shows every measured consequence at once: contrast, scrim and ink.
- **Reflow.**
  - 1920: a 1,200 measure with specimens three across.
  - 834: two across.
  - 390: one column, the scale in two columns of six, and the heroes as a snapping strip.
- **What Northside changes.** Each plate is one setting.

**D · "The crew"**
- **Composition.** A band opens the page: Northside's mark on its chosen hero, scrimmed from its own pixels, with the trading name. Under it:
  - the people as **posters**, three across, each the name large with title, phone and email as the card prints, and the one switch *May change prices and settings* with its sentence;
  - "Add a person" as the fourth slot;
  - the details as Northside's business card, opening into the letterhead preview;
  - the doors as tiles that name what each holds ("Rules · 7 clauses");
  - appearance last.
- **Order.** Who → what they print → where else to go → how it looks.
- **Exclusive references.** `live/apple-ios17-contact-poster.png` and `live/hubspot-signature.png`. Tone, not exclusive: `live/northside-contact.png`.
- **Only D** makes three people a page rather than a table, and feeds the door its known names.
- **Reflow.**
  - 1920: four posters across.
  - 834: two by two.
  - 390: a snap carousel at 86% width with the next poster peeking, and the band shrinks to the mark and the name.
- **What Northside changes.** The band's photograph, the mark, the accent, and each person's card.

**Beyond the brief.** D's poster could also be the door's list of names, so sign-in offers faces. The owner decides.

## 6. What the seed can honestly put here

- **Marks.** Northside's dark (`#0066cc`) and white marks, with provenance and "Owner to confirm". Mercury and Stabicraft are refused on paper, as white-only.
- **Pictures.** The eight `person: false` heroes, as candidates for the door and Home, each with its model named. Every other picture belongs to its exact model and is never a background. An upload is recorded as Northside's own.
- **Details.** The price file's business name ("Northside Marine") and `OrgProfile` as it stands: name, industry, createdAt, slug and quoteTerms. Everything else starts empty and prints nothing. The proof PDF's address and phone are not offered, because there is no OWNER 4 yes.
- **People.** None exist as records, because the session holds only the first name typed at the door. The block starts as a sentence and "Add a person".
- **The preview.** The most recent real draft, or the sentence. Never a fixture.
- **Home.** Its sections as `hl2-home` built them, in its order: the sea, the desk, "Start from the boat" and "What {business} sells".
