# Picker and Place — the sweep

Driven 2026-09-17. **76 sources · 72 frames · 70 distinct images · 39 dead, walled or redirected**,
so 37 usable. Every frame cited was opened and looked at; the census, the failures and the two
byte-identical pairs are in `sources-index.md`. Paths are relative to `docs/research/refs/picker/`
unless they name `home/` (a prior sweep, re-read) or `…\hl-refs\ref\` (the stock).

**The job, restated against the file.** Counted from `manifest.json` and `entities.json`: **seven
boat tables · 810 hulls · 42 named series · 289 models · 792 rows with a cash figure, 18 without**
(all nine Haines Signature rows, nine of thirty-nine Formosa).

## 0. The hard question, answered from the file before any reference

588 rows is not 588 things. Measured on `boat_highfield`:

- **7 series → 67 models → 588 variants.** Roll-Up 8 models, Ultralite 8, Classic 16, Coaster 1,
  Patrol 16, Sport 16, Adventure 2. A median of 9 variants per model, never fewer than 4.
- **The variant is material × colourway, and the material is what costs money.** The SP560 has
  fifteen variants and two prices: PVC $41,340 across seven colourways, HYP $48,350 across eight.
  **In 43 of the 67 models the price is a function of material alone**; in the other 24 it splits
  further inside a material (every Roll-Up: `RU230KAM` is PVC $2,770, HYP $4,500 *and* $5,320). The
  collapse is real but not universal — a board may not assume it, and the screen must show the figure
  against the variant wherever the file disagrees with itself. 52 models carry both materials, 13 are
  HYP only, 1 PVC only.
- **The colourway is 17 undecoded code tokens** — `B C DB DG G I LB LG MB O R ST W WB WD WG WH`.
  `docs/STATUS.md` question 3 says `I`, `O`, `R`, `WH` have no decode map and are a question for the
  dealer, never a guess. **No swatch can be drawn.** The code is the content.

So: **810 → 289 model cards → two material chips and a written colourway count.**

A second axis is honest: **808 of 810 hulls carry a numeric hull length**, and all seven tables carry
Hull Length, Beam, Min HP and Max HP. The distribution is in §5, direction D.

## 3. Type and motion, as observed

Two-tone at one size is where the reading is fastest: Porsche's grey ~12 px label under a ~22 px
figure, Grady-White's LENGTH over 25', Surtees' OVERALL LENGTH over 7700MM. Scale contrast is modest
— Porsche's rail heading to body is barely 1.5×, Framework's "563 results" is *smaller* than the rows
it heads. The big type goes on the name of the thing, not the page title: Stabicraft's 2100 at
~64 px red over SUPERCAB, Highfield's THE SPORT RANGE at ~56 px lower-left on the water. One hue used
once — Lotus's yellow DISCOVER, Framework's orange Configure now — answers "a bit more colour usage"
without a palette.

**Motion: no board may claim an easing or a duration from a still.** The stills license one
observation — the selection is a short coloured mark travelling along a ladder: red under `2750`
(`stabicraft-size-ranges.png`), red under `429` (`stacer-seamasters.png`), red under CENTER CONSOLES
(`gradywhite-models.png`), black fill on the active pill (`apple-buy-mac.png`), an orange bar at the
active category's left edge (`framework-marketplace.png`). One travelling indicator, not a change of
ground.

## 4. What to avoid, each with its frame

- **An empty chooser with no sentence** — `zodiac-comparator.png`: three identical blue "Your boat"
  selects and four hundred pixels of white below them.
- **A dimmed act with nothing saying what would enable it** — `home/live2/mastercraft-boat-finder.png`
  (CONTINUE, greyed, silent). The softer version: `porsche-models.png` writes the reason *above* the
  control without tying it to it, so a screen reader reaches a dead button with no name.
- **A personal-data gate before the catalogue** — `rivian-configurations.png` (postal code over
  skeleton cards), `whaler-boats.png` (a newsletter modal wanting name, email, country and postcode
  over "Our Boat Models"). Nothing was typed in either.
- **Two ways to lose a seven-item row** — `…\ref\boats\zodiac-2.png` hides seven ranges behind a
  seven-dot pager; `…\ref\boats\highfield-2.png` puts four across 1440 and the Roll Up and the
  Ultralite become indistinguishable grey dinghies. Our Highfield series row is seven.
- **A floating act that eats the words underneath** — `stacer-range2.png`, `stacer-seamasters.png`:
  the BUILD MY BOAT pill sits over the paragraph's first words on both.
- **A consent card with no honest refusal** — `beneteau-power.png` (CONTINUE WITHOUT ACCEPTING as a
  small underlined link while two filled buttons sit below), `bmw-all-models.png` (Analytics and
  Marketing pre-toggled to "Allowed"), `gradywhite-models.png`, `saxdor-models.png`, `lucid-air.png`.
- **Believing the manufacturer's taxonomy** — `stacer-range2.png` claims "over 70 models" and "9
  ranges"; the file carries 91 rows in 22 series. `highfield-classic-range.png`'s nav ships Velox and
  ADV ranges the file does not carry, and the file carries Coaster the nav does not show. **The
  picker's tiers are the file's, and the count beside them is a row count.**

## 5. Four directions — four different orders, not four grounds

**A — "The counted rail."** *Order:* facets first. A left rail of brands with counts, expanding into
that brand's series with counts, then collapsed facets for length band, HP envelope and price; a grid
of model cards on the stage. *Grouping:* the file's hierarchy, every group carrying its row count,
all 810 addressable without leaving the page.
**Exclusive to A:** `porsche-models.png` (counted radios headed "All (72)") and
`framework-marketplace.png` (a count on every row under "563 results"). No other board needs a number
on every row.

**B — "Index and stage."** *Order:* everything named first, one thing shown. One left column lists
brand ▸ series ▸ model as plain type — 42 series and 289 models down a single scroll — and the right
two-thirds shows only the row under the cursor: its picture *or* its drawn outline, its fact strip,
its from-price, its act. *Grouping:* typographic weight alone; no cards, no tiles, no photograph
needed for the screen to be complete.
**Exclusive to B:** `home/live/saxdor-models.png` (fifteen models, six headings, one column, no
pictures) and `deantonio-builder.png` (a chooser made only of line drawings). Only B holds 289 models
without a grid, and only B survives a brand with no photography.

**C — "Three tiers at once."** *Order:* brand, model, facts — side by side and all live. A narrow
rail of seven brands (mark where the ledger has one, name where it does not); a middle column of that
brand's series and models with the from-price right-aligned; a right panel with the model's picture,
fact strip, material chips and their two prices, the colourway count and **Start the quote**.
*Grouping:* three columns, one question each; changing brand re-fills the panel, never blanks it.
**Exclusive to C:** `home/live/williams-tenders.png` (rail, size column and fact panel in one window)
and `surtees-770.png` (the three-fact strip with the act at its right end). Only C keeps all three
tiers on screen, so only it needs a panel design.

**D — "How big, then whose."** *Order:* the measurement first, the brand last. A length ladder across
all seven brands *is* the navigation (2–3 m 99 · 3–4 m 198 · 4–5 m 111 · 5–6 m 129 · 6–7 m 153 ·
7–8 m 72 · 8–9 m 21 · 9–10 m 17, plus four bands above), with a count that falls as the dealer adds
the HP envelope and a price band; brand is a facet, not a door. *Grouping:* by measurement, so a
5.37 m Stacer SeaMaster and a 5.6 m Highfield SP560 sit in one band — which no brand's own site can
do and the dealer does every day.
**Exclusive to D:** `stabicraft-size-ranges.png` and `stabicraft-style-series.png`, the only
published proof that a length ladder can *be* the navigation, plus
`home/live2/mastercraft-boat-finder.png` for a counter that starts at zero and is printed anyway. No
other board sorts across brands.

## 6. Imagery: what the seed can honestly put here

Counted 2026-09-17 from `images.json`, `heroes-ledger.json` and `marks-ledger.json`:

| table | rows | rows with an address | distinct held files |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stacer | 91 | 82 | 50 |
| Stabicraft | 37 | 37 | 18 |
| Surtees | 19 | 18 | 17 |
| Jeanneau | 27 | 27 | 26 |
| Haines Signature | 9 | 8 | 7 |
| Highfield | 588 | 534 | **115** |
| Formosa | 39 | **18** | 17 |

1. **Highfield has 115 held pictures for 588 rows and not one is a scene** — against Jeanneau 27 of
   27 held rows scenes, Stabicraft 33 of 37, Stacer 40 of 60. 292 Highfield rows resolve to a held
   render; **32 of the 67 models have no held picture at all**, and none has exactly one — so a model
   card cannot assume a picture and a colourway cannot assume its own.
2. **A brand tile cannot be a photograph.** Only two of seven brands have a stage picture — four
   Highfield (SP560, SP600, PA600, ADV7, all 2560 px) and four Stacer (519 Sea Ranger 2560 × 1694,
   481 SeaMaster, 309 Skimma, 359 Territory Striker); the catalogue tier caps at long edge 1100.
   **Only six of the seven have a mark**, Stabicraft's ledger row reading `"no public wordmark
   verified"`. So the brand step falls back in a stated order: a held picture, else the mark
   (`porsche-finder.png`), else the name or a drawing set large (`deantonio-builder.png`).
3. **No colourway swatch.** The variant step shows the material as a chip with its price and the
   colourway as the file's own string in mono — B&O's "5 Colours" written, not Apple's named swatches.

A drawn side profile is the only treatment that works for all 289 models today. The dealer's own mark
is in the sweep — `northside-new-boats.png`, white wave chevrons on navy with the yellow script
tagline — but `marks-ledger.json` holds suppliers only, so a board that enlarges *Northside's* mark
draws an asset the repo cannot ship, and must say so on its face. And because a second dealership
replaces the photographs and the mark, **every direction must be legible with the picture column
empty**: B passes outright, C with its panel, A and D must answer it.
