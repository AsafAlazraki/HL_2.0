# HL_2.0 — rebuild plan

## Context

**What we are building.** HL_2.0 (GitHub `AsafAlazraki/HL_2.0`, public, empty; local folder `C:\Users\Asaf\Desktop\HL 2.0`, empty, not yet a git repo) is a from-scratch rebuild of HL_Playground ("HelmLogic Dynamic Config", local checkout `C:\Users\Asaf\dev\HL_Playground`, branch `rebuild` = `main` at `4f4861b`). The old app is a local-first configurator for a boat dealership's data model: one table per brand, English-sentence business rules, motor/trailer fitment with reasons recorded at removal, quotes with freeze-on-pick pricing, modules with capabilities and roles, a CRM, a pipeline, import/export, a review linter, a whiteboard ERD, a dense sheet. It holds Northside Marine's real Master Price File as seed: 53 tables, 15,691 rows (28 tables are fitment joins holding 8,679 pairings), plus 220 local product images. Its lineage: the original production HelmLogic (Next.js + Firebase, used by the dealership) → HL_Playground (the dynamic-config experiment) → HL_2.0 (this rebuild, which takes the best of both).

**Why a rebuild.** Five redesign passes on the old repo were rejected on sight; the owner then spent weeks without visible progress. The old repo's own diagnosis (docs/plan/REBUILD.md, DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §9): a rulebook that made restraint the goal, docs that lagged the tree so sessions rebuilt what already existed, and guards that measured tidiness rather than beauty. The bones are good (the engine has 2,974 passing tests); the interface and the working method are not.

**The bar.** The owner judges by eye against the best boat configurators, driven live and captured in `C:\Users\Asaf\dev\HL_Playground\out\ref\boats` (Saxdor chapters, Axopar split stage, Nimbus photo tiles, Zodiac, De Antonio, Beneteau, Jeanneau, Riviera), `out\ref` (Porsche configurator + PDF) and `out\ref\entry` (Porsche/BMW/Polestar/Tesla/Linear logins). "It is not just Porsche — other boat places have stunning configurators… I want every new section genuinely beautiful."

## Decisions settled with the owner (2026-09-16)

1. **Backend: local-first, backend-ready.** IndexedDB today behind one repository seam; a Supabase/Postgres adapter is a later phase, not a rewrite. Single user for now.
2. **Seed: repo stays public; commit the full seed and images.** (Noted once: the seed carries a real dealer's cost columns; HL_Playground already publishes them.)
3. **Scope: selling flow first.** Milestone 1 = entry/sign-in, home, quote picker, place, chaptered configurator, cascade, the document, the quotes register. Cockpit (sheet, rules, fitment, customers, pipeline, modules, roles, admin, import/export, review, levels, whiteboard) follows.
   **Added by the owner the same day:** the original HelmLogic's **modules with cover images and logos**, its **organisation and user management**, and its **quote/document templates** (content blocks with the override cascade, the branded PDF) are IN scope as later milestones. Stock, contracts, deposits, promotions, rego lines and landed cost stay on the "later" list.
4. **Design process: directions first, after research.** For each major screen: a reference sweep well beyond Porsche (boat and car configurators, premium build-and-price flows, the best dashboards and registers), then 2–3 static directions published as a design canvas; the owner picks; then build.
5. **Imagery is a first-class workstream.** The owner: "imagery is insanely important… you gotta find the right ones, they are all publicly available… like the original HelmLogic we should have cover images." Every brand, series, model and module gets the right public picture, with provenance, before its screen is called done.

## Non-negotiable: the old design system does not come across

The owner, 2026-09-16: weeks in the old repo went nowhere because every session "would just use the same boring design system and things". So, explicitly:

- **Nothing visual is ported.** Not `src/styles/system.css`, `world.css`, `ds.css`, `tokens.css`, not the `--t-*` ramp, not the accent keys, not the tile rule, not the entry frame, not a single stylesheet or `.tsx` from the old repo. No "Quiet Precision" heritage, no Porsche-only language.
- **The old rulebook is not ported.** No `CLAUDE.md` constitution, no `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, no literal-px ratchet, no orphan-class baseline, no lint-warning ceiling. The new repo's `CLAUDE.md` stays under one screen and is about honesty and safety (no fake data, no invented figures, cost never customer-facing), never about restraint.
- **What is kept is the engine only**: the domain contract, pricing/freeze/totals, the solver, fitment with recorded reasons, the seed and its images, and the node tests that pin them.
- **Every screen is designed from the reference captures, per section**, with 2–3 static directions published before it is built, and judged by eye in the browser, not by a ruler.

- "The original HelmLogic's quote flow is the thing stakeholders loved" — the step rail with progress is the one thing it does better and it comes back.

**Two standing tensions, settled by the process rather than by a rule:** (1) the old spec demanded ≥6× scale contrast on Showroom screens and the owner called the 6× head "awful" — HL_2.0 measures scale contrast and reports it, but the owner's eye on the direction board decides the size; (2) the owner once asked for a bottom action bar ("Reminder… asked for before and not done") and later called the quote flow's bottom bar "disgusting" — actions live where they act by default, and any Cockpit screen may offer a page-level action bar as one of its directions for the owner to pick or reject.
- "Never invent a figure the price file does not carry."
- Cost and margin never reach a customer-facing surface. A refusal is a sentence with a reason where it is refused. Undo over confirm. Contrast measured. The price figure never counts up.
- Finish a phase, then start the next without asking. Push only when told. No `/loop`.

Synthesised from the eight-area survey and a three-way design panel (port-maximalist · clean-slate · design-engineering-first) scored by three judges. ### UI kit rules (learned from what broke in the old shell)

- **One dialog and one popover primitive before any screen** (the old app hand-rolled 14 dialogs and 8 popovers and copied a focus-trap selector five times; return-focus-to-opener was already broken in production). Base UI supplies focus trap, dismissal and portal; `src/ui` wraps each once.
- **Primitives refuse `className` and `style`, and the rule is enforced mechanically** (a lint bans `.ui-` selectors outside `src/ui`); the old app's ten feature stylesheets reached into primitive internals through parent selectors.
- **Every register token has a `:root` default and every route root declares its register** (a guard fails otherwise); the old app had 17 declarations of `var(--row-h)` with no fallback and 15 of ~188 components declaring a register.
- **Load exactly the faces the tokens name and prove it** (a guard cross-checks `@font-face` against the font-family tokens in the cascade); the old app shipped Inter, Archivo and Bricolage that nothing referenced.
- **Light theme only until a theme is first-class**; no half-scoped dark mode.
- **Route-level lazy loading from the first route**; the old shell statically imported every stage and both generations of six screens.
- **The Escape precedence ladder is a pure, tested function** (a widget that owns the keyboard > a field owns its own Escape > an already-handled event is not ours > otherwise close), ported from `stageKeys.ts`.
- **A refusal is never a disabled control**: `Button` takes `refusedBecause` and renders the sentence beneath with `aria-disabled`, because a disabled button cannot be focused, hovered or read by a screen reader.
- **Chrome is charged to a page only when used**: a page with no actions renders no bar and pays no height.
- **The sheet is a pure `GridModel` selector plus a dumb renderer**, never a 2,279-line component again; `table/core/**`, `sections.ts`, `grouping.ts` and `helpers.ts` port byte-for-byte with their one shared idea (folding a band or a drawer removes cells from the addressable set, so keyboard navigation, paste and fill never learn that sections exist) and their measured reason for two-axis virtualisation. The 18-row density requirement is stated per table shape (a two-level hierarchy costs two lines per four rows) before the grid is written.
- **A canvas container always has a box while mounted, and its camera lives outside the component** (React Flow in a `display:none` container emitted `<pattern x="NaN">`).
- **Nothing moves while a caret is in a text field, app-wide** (the old stillness gate was mounted on one screen of five, so toasts reflowed and lists re-sorted under a typing dealer); reduced motion keeps colour and opacity and removes movement.
- **No boot-time migrations or adoptions.** The old app ran six one-shot adoptions at start-up to repair keys and registries it had outgrown; a fresh schema with `orgId` on every record and one versioned pack needs none, and the first one added is a design smell to argue about.
- **A select is never native**: the old app's own hand-built listbox lists what a native `<select>` gives free and must be repaid (arrows, Home/End, Escape, type-ahead, `aria-expanded`, focus back to the trigger); Base UI's Select pays it once.

### State and persistence

- `state/catalogue.ts`: `{status, version, tables, rows, index}`; read-only in M1; M2 adds named commands (updateCell, addRow, deleteRow, addColumn, rename, retype with counted blast radius) with inverses. The Dexie adapter keeps the old identity-diff ledger inside it (a cell edit writes one row, not a 2,500-row table; the old repo measured the wholesale write at ~10 s). Undo is `commit(op, fn)`: an entry is kept only if the mutation ran (fixes the old phantom-entry defect). **A sheet wipe never clears quotes** ("Your 3 quotes stay. A quote is a photograph of what was offered on the day"); a separate, explicit "forget everything" in Admin does.
